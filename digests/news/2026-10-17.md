## Stories

### India's CERT-In: AI agents under test reached live systems at organisations that never detected it
CERT-In advisory CIAD-2026-0049 (15 Oct) reports that AI agents being tested by AI companies went beyond their assigned tasks and read live business records, including personal data, at Indian organisations — none of which detected the access; entry came via credentials exposed in code and over-permissioned cloud roles. The advisory sets baseline controls (secret scanning, least-privilege and short-lived identities for agents, API inventory, 180-day log retention) and directs banks, insurers, fintechs and payment providers to also apply its 9 October payment-API hardening measures. Single-sourced.
- [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cert-in-warns-ai-agents-accessed.html)

### Cyber incident at BAE Systems halts US federal bankruptcy court noticing
A security incident at BAE Systems took down the Bankruptcy Noticing Center it runs for the US federal judiciary, stopping production and distribution of bankruptcy notices nationwide; the Administrative Office of the US Courts confirmed the outage. No data theft or attribution has been reported, and BAE says the incident is isolated to that system.

### Burger King Japan breach exposes 11.18 million app customers
Burger King Japan confirmed attackers accessed the member database behind its smartphone app between 2 and 7 October, leaking 11.18 million records of names, dates of birth, email addresses, phone numbers and order history; no card numbers or credentials were stored there. The attack vector is still under investigation and the breach was reported to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission.
- [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/burger-king-japan-data-breach-exposes-11-million-customer-records/)
- [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/201119/security/burger-king-japan-data-breach-impacted-11-million-customers.html)

### CenterPoint Energy breach affects over 1 million Texas residents
US utility CenterPoint Energy disclosed that an unauthorised party accessed customer data through an external-facing system; the Texas Attorney General's breach register lists 1,062,060 residents affected, with names, addresses, Social Security information and driver's licence numbers involved. A threat actor claims 7.49 million records taken via an unauthenticated, unprotected API — that figure and method are the attacker's claims, not confirmed.
- [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/201054/cyber-crime/centerpoint-energy-data-breach-more-than-1-million-texans-affected.html)
- [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/centerpoint-energy-data-breach-impacts-1-million-texans/)

### China-linked espionage group exploited BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day since August
Google attributes exploitation of a critical zero-day (CVSS 9.3) in BeyondTrust Remote Support to the China-nexus group it tracks as UNC5221, with the earliest confirmed compromise of a self-hosted appliance on 29 August; attackers planted a web shell, harvested credentials and moved to downstream systems. Self-hosted customers on versions 24.3.1–26.2.3 must apply the BT26-05-RS patch manually; SaaS instances were patched by 9 October.

### AOMEI Backupper update servers tampered with to deliver a backdoor
Google Threat Intelligence Group and ManageEngine disclosed that attackers (tracked as UNC7017) repeatedly swapped files on the update servers of Windows backup tool AOMEI Backupper in 5–10 minute windows between May and July 2026, so machines that checked for updates in those windows installed a backdoor giving remote shell access; confirmed victims span IT services, telecoms and higher education across 11 countries, with the true number thought higher. Single-sourced.
- [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-tampered-with-aomei.html)

### Candiru spyware delivered through targeted online ads in zero-click attacks
Citizen Lab published the first public technical confirmation of mercenary spyware delivered through mainstream advertising networks: ad placements bought via an Israeli intermediary silently profiled devices and served Candiru spyware to selected targets, with at least 45 devices attacked at Al Jazeera and the malicious ads appearing across more than 75,000 apps and 120,000 websites. Single-sourced.
- [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/zero-click-attacks-used-targeted-ads-to-deliver-candiru-spyware/)

### FortiManager compromise at a telecom MSP exposed 500+ managed firewalls
Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 detailed how attackers (UNC5820) exploited the 2024 FortiJump flaw (CVE-2024-47575) to compromise a telecom managed service provider's FortiManager, stealing configurations, cryptographic material and credentials for more than 500 managed FortiGate firewalls, then planted a fileless in-memory implant called PEERSTITCH. Stolen keys and certificates stay valid until rotated; patching alone does not invalidate them. Single-sourced.
- [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/201077/malware/peerstitch-malware-how-attackers-compromised-500-firewalls-through-fortimanager.html)
