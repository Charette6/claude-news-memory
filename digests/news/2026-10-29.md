## Breach of GMO's FX-trading cloud reaches broker customer accounts
GMO Internet confirmed attackers compromised its Onamae.com Desktop Cloud — Windows desktops used for retail FX trading — stole customer information, demanded roughly $50 million, and threatened more than 70 securities and FX firms; 26,739 servers are counted as potentially affected. Brokers including OANDA Japan and FXTF linked unauthorized trades on customer accounts to logins leaked from the service, and GMO is compensating customers and opened a bounty of up to $1 million on 28 October.

## US sanctions network laundering North Korean IT-worker earnings
The US Treasury (OFAC) on 28 October sanctioned two Russian nationals, several North Korean individuals and a North Korean company for laundering cryptocurrency earned by North Korean IT workers hired under stolen or false identities at US and European companies. Alleged flows per designee range from $100,000 to about $3.5 million. (Single-sourced.)

- https://therecord.media/north-korea-russia-sanctions

## Japan Airlines customer data exposed through reservations provider Amadeus
Japan Airlines said hackers accessed reservation data of about 92,000 customers through Amadeus, the third-party provider running its reservation, ticketing and check-in systems; for about 2,000 of them passport numbers and contact details were also taken. The airline says card numbers were not compromised and flight operations were unaffected. (Single-sourced.)

- https://therecord.media/japan-airlines-warns-of-breach-country-grapples-with-incidents

## Sony's Japanese ISP confirms records of 2.5 million customers stolen
Sony Network Communications said an unauthorized party accessed its So-net member database on 19 October, exposing 2,585,481 current and former customer records (names, contact details, dates of birth) plus passwords for about 54,000 email accounts. The company says no banking or card data was taken; NTT Docomo customers are affected via a reseller arrangement.

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sony-isp-subsidiary-data-breach-exposes-25-million-customer-records/
- https://therecord.media/japan-airlines-warns-of-breach-country-grapples-with-incidents

## Engie notifies French customers after marketing supplier compromised
French energy group Engie told an undisclosed number of customers that attackers accessed their data through a third-party marketing platform used by its partners; exposed fields include names, addresses, phone numbers and energy contract identifiers, with no banking data per the company. Engie says its own systems were not compromised and the incident is contained. (Single-sourced.)

- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/energy-giant-engie-supply-chain/

## Volt Typhoon ran four zero-day campaigns through a covert relay network
Lumen's Black Lotus Labs disrupted "Silent Train", a relay network of compromised routers and IoT devices that China-linked Volt Typhoon used to hide four zero-day campaigns against Versa Director, N-able N-central, Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN and Arista VeloCloud management platforms. The researchers count hundreds of victims across telecoms, government and critical infrastructure.

- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/volt-typhoon-used-covert-silent-train.html
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/volt-typhoon-hides-zero-day-attacks-using-silent-train-framework/

## FBI and European police seize Virtualine bulletproof hosting service
The FBI, with Dutch, German and UK police, seized the primary domain of bulletproof host Virtualine and dozens of physical servers in the Netherlands, taking hundreds of virtual servers used for ransomware, phishing and infostealer operations offline. Authorities say they recovered data on more than 3,000 of its customers for follow-up action.
