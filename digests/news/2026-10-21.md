# Security News Digest — 2026-10-21

Public record of security news published in the 24 hours to 2026-10-21 08:03 (UTC+3).

## Financial sector

### Revolut down worldwide for hours after a Google Cloud power failure in London
Revolut lost most of its services on Monday morning when Google Cloud's London region (europe-west2) suffered a power event; its status page listed 28 services as partially unavailable, including sign-ins, card payments and transfers. Services were restored the same day, and Revolut said all customer money and data were safe — an infrastructure outage, not an attack.

## Regulatory & supervisory

### UK FCA: significant AI failures and near-misses should be reported like major incidents
The FCA's chief data officer said in a speech that existing incident-reporting rules for financial firms should extend to significant AI failures and near-misses. This is a stated supervisory expectation, not yet a consultation or rule, with no thresholds or timelines given (single source).
- https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/20/uk-finance-watchdog-wants-firms-to-report-ai-failures-and-close-calls/5304078

### 23andMe's successor pays California $6M over the 2023 breach
Chrome Holding Company, formerly 23andMe, settled with California's attorney general for $6 million over the security failings behind the 2023 breach that exposed data of nearly 7 million people, following an $18 million settlement with 42 other states in July. The deal preserves consumers' rights to delete data and destroy genetic samples, pending bankruptcy-court approval (single source).
- https://therecord.media/23andme-california-data-breach-settlement

## Major incidents

### Certificate authority eMudhra's keys were trivially crackable; browsers rushed out blocks
Eight eMudhra intermediate certificates were revoked after a researcher found their private keys could be recovered almost instantly — a script error in April 2025 generated extremely weak key values. Mozilla, Google and Apple blocked the certificates between 17 and 19 October and Go shipped an emergency release; eMudhra says there is no evidence the keys were abused.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/mozilla-google-apple-block-compromised-emudhra-ca-certificates/
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/eight-emudhra-ca-keys-compromised.html
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/20/indian-certification-authority-emudhras-private-keys-compromised-one-of-them-was-the-number-4/5304312
- https://www.darkreading.com/cyber-risk/tech-giants-emudhra-certificates-compromised-keys

### Nikkei discloses its third breach in a month — 1.83 million records
Attackers used stolen credentials to access a development system at the Japanese financial-news group and exposed 1.83 million records, about 150,000 of which held names, contact details or dates of birth; no card data was involved. It is Nikkei's third disclosed incident in roughly a month, after compromises of an employee Google Workspace account and a Microsoft 365 account.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/nikkei-discloses-third-data-breach-in-a-month-18m-records-exposed/
- https://www.securityweek.com/hackers-steal-1-8-million-records-from-nikkei/
- https://securityaffairs.com/201369/data-breach/nikkei-data-breach-affected-over-1-8-million-records.html

### ConnectWise suspends cloud services after attacker accessed backup application identities
ConnectWise confirmed unauthorised access to application identities tied to its Cloud Backup service and suspended Cloud Backup, SaaS Security and Cloud Management while it resets credentials; partners are told to review their Microsoft 365 tenants and not reconnect until cleared. Researchers at Huntress traced the vector to a compromised vendor credential and found persistence via a self-granted Global Administrator role in one tenant.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/connectwise-resets-credentials-as-cloud.html
- https://www.securityweek.com/connectwise-suspends-cloud-services-in-response-to-cyberattack/

## Active campaigns

### Ransomware groups exploiting Palo Alto GlobalProtect to bypass MFA since spring
Qilin, INC and Settra ransomware operators are exploiting an authentication-bypass flaw in PAN-OS GlobalProtect portals (CVE-2026-0257, patched in May) to establish VPN sessions without MFA; Unit 42 has investigated resulting intrusions since early June across sectors including finance. Rapid7 observed successful exploitation as early as 17 May.
- https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/10/20/palo-altos-firewall-os-has-been-under-attack-from-multiple-ransomware-groups-since-june/5304226
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/qilin-and-inc-ransomware-groups-exploit.html
- https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/active-exploitation-of-pan-os-cve-2026-0257/

### FBI: SafePay ransomware has hit more than 600 organisations since August 2024
A joint advisory from the FBI and German and UK agencies calls SafePay among the most active ransomware groups of 2026, with at least 600 victims across the US and Europe. Entry is via password guessing, phishing and known VPN and backup-software flaws, with data exfiltrated over remote-desktop tools before encryption (single source).
- https://therecord.media/fbi-says-safepay-ransomware-gang-among-most-active-600-victims

### Exposed AI attack platform used to automate intrusions at scale
Researchers at Norforge found exposed control panels of the open-source AI agent platform Cairn being used to automate attacks on hundreds of websites, the largest cluster running over 1,100 attack projects against e-commerce and payment infrastructure. Roughly 10% of attempts succeeded, and Reuters reports more than 100 companies were breached.
