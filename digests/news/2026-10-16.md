## Financial sector

### Android trojan uninstalls real banking apps and installs trojanized copies
ThreatFabric detailed Hirudo, an Android RAT that shows a fake update prompt, removes the victim's genuine banking app and installs a tampered copy, using hooking to evade signature, screen-capture and Play Integrity checks. Eight Indonesian banking apps are targeted today; internal code references banks in China, India, Mexico, Morocco and Thailand, with no victim counts published.
- https://www.securityweek.com/novel-android-banking-rat-hirudo-replaces-banking-apps-with-trojanized-copies/
- https://securityaffairs.com/200970/malware/hirudo-android-trojan-replaces-banking-apps-to-steal-credentials.html

### Bank of America brought in 1,000 contractors to patch AI-discovered bugs
On its Q3 earnings call, CEO Brian Moynihan said the bank added roughly 1,000 contractors in Q2 to remediate vulnerabilities identified by Anthropic's Mythos model, putting combined Mythos and anti-money-laundering remediation costs at "a couple of hundred million plus." The AML side includes a $1.21bn FinCEN settlement and a $750m OCC fine, and shares fell over 8% after the disclosure. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/bank-of-america-had-to-bring-in-1000-contractors-to-patch-all-the-bugs-mythos-identified/5303275

## Regulatory & supervisory

### UK ICO reprimands Metropolitan Police over use of an unapproved third-party platform
The ICO issued a reprimand and enforcement notice after Met officers used the Auror retail-crime platform for 18 months without formal approval or a data protection impact assessment; about 500 accounts were created and personal data on 179 people was uploaded. The ICO's message: innovation cannot outpace data protection governance.
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ico-reprimands-met-over-thirdparty/
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/metropolitan-polices-unchecked-use-of-shoplifting-platform-lands-force-in-trouble-with-data-watchdog/5303061

## Major incidents elsewhere

### UK Legal Aid Agency hack far larger than first disclosed, with records back to 1978
Forensic review shows the May 2025 breach took records well beyond the original "since 2010" disclosure: about 250,000 records predate 2007, the earliest dates from 1978, and stolen data includes probation records, judges' and lawyers' names, financial assessments, bank account details and partially redacted card data. Attackers claimed data on over 2 million people; that figure is unconfirmed.
- https://therecord.media/uk-legal-aid-cyberattack-data
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/legal-aid-agency-hackers-made-off-with-records-dating-back-to-1978/5303243
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/uk-legal-aid-agency-hackers-stole-records-dating-back-to-1978/
- https://www.securityweek.com/uk-legal-aid-agency-hack-scope-widens-after-forensic-review/

### Refrigeration sabotage hit about 700 French supermarkets; attack widens to more vendors
Attackers manipulated internet-exposed refrigeration controllers at roughly 700 French stores (per the finance minister), stretching defrost cycles to delay alarms, spoiling food and in some cases causing fires; CERT-FR has added Copeland and Dixell systems to its Danfoss alert, and stores in Spain, Portugal, Poland, Germany and Belgium were also reportedly affected. Most hit devices were end-of-life units no longer receiving patches, and operators appear to be disconnecting rather than patching: internet-exposed French panels fell from over 1,800 to 537 in three days.
- https://www.securityweek.com/supermarket-refrigeration-attack-widens-to-copeland-dixell-controllers/
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/researchers-warned-attackers-could-defrost-the-food-months-before-french-supermarkets-got-hit/5303425
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/france-expose-danfoss-controllers/
- https://www.darkreading.com/ics-ot-security/eu-grocers-unpatched-fridge-firmware

### US federal bankruptcy courts' noticing system down after cyberattack on contractor BAE Systems
A security incident at BAE Systems took down the Bankruptcy Noticing Center, which produces and sends notices for all US federal bankruptcy courts; notice production has stopped and several uscourts.gov services are unavailable. No data theft has been confirmed. (Single-sourced.)
- https://therecord.media/federal-bankruptcy-courts-dealing-with-cyberattack-outage

## Active campaigns

### Zero-click spyware delivered through ordinary online ads; Al Jazeera among targets
Citizen Lab published the first technical confirmation that mercenary spyware (Candiru) infected real devices through targeted ads with no user interaction: an ad firm screened devices by advertising ID and IP, and only selected targets received attack code. At least 45 Al Jazeera devices were targeted in 2024–2025; Google and IAB Europe have since removed the ad firm.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/citizen-lab-traces-zero-click-candiru.html
- https://therecord.media/spyware-citizen-lab-advertising
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/spyware-vendors-found-a-way-to-make-targeted-ads-even-worse/5303334
- https://securityaffairs.com/201015/hacking/adfection-beyond-the-click-citizen-lab-exposes-ad-delivered-spyware-networks.html

### Microsoft disrupts phishing marketplace behind over a billion scam messages
Microsoft, with law enforcement partners, disabled more than 20,000 accounts and blocked over 100,000 domains tied to Yam Store, a Chinese-language phishing-as-a-service marketplace whose kits sent over a billion phishing emails and texts to more than 75,000 organisations since January 2026. Financial services is a confirmed target sector, and the kits were built to steal payment card data.
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/microsoft-yam-store-one-billion/
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/15/microsoft-disrupting-phishing-kits-marketplace-yam-store/
