## Financial sector

### AI-driven intrusion campaign stole data from South Korean financial firms
CrowdStrike reports that a financially motivated, likely Chinese-speaking actor used ARTEX, an open-source AI penetration-testing tool from China, against several South Korean financial organisations from late September to early October, reaching one bank's loan-inquiry service and another's employee mobile work-support system. CrowdStrike's analysis found data was stolen, but no victim is named and none has confirmed a breach.
- https://securityaffairs.com/200661/hacking/ai-driven-tool-artex-used-in-attacks-against-south-korean-banks.html
- https://www.securityweek.com/in-other-news-ai-used-in-korean-bank-breaches-poem-guided-botnet-empire-admin-gets-40-years/

### Money-mule network boss pleads guilty in the US
Oleg Korniev, a principal of the "Your Mule Cashout" operation, pleaded guilty in US federal court to laundering at least $10 million for hackers who broke into 750 US bank accounts. The network recruited more than 15,000 unwitting mules through fake job websites between 2007 and 2014, forwarding stolen funds to Eastern Europe via Western Union and MoneyGram.
- https://therecord.media/leader-of-money-mule-operation-for-cybercriminals-pleads-guilty
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ukrainian-russian-dual-citizen-admits-to-laundering-millions-for-cybercriminals/

## Regulatory & supervisory

### UK privacy regulator extracts data-protection pledges from ten AI firms
The UK Information Commissioner's Office said Amazon, Anthropic, Apple, Cohere, DeepSeek, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI and Stability AI have committed to clearer transparency and stronger user-rights mechanisms around model training, and it opened formal investigations into X and xAI over Grok's data processing. It also launched a six-week call for evidence on agentic AI, closing 20 November 2026, feeding a future statutory code of practice. (Single source.)
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ai-firms-pledge-data-protection/

## Major incidents elsewhere

### Cardiac-monitoring firm iRhythm: breach affected at least 360,000 people
US medical device maker iRhythm says attackers used social engineering to access third-party-hosted business applications between 3 and 8 June 2026 and downloaded patient data including names, insurance details and account numbers; state filings put the count at at least 360,000 (Texas 298,647; South Carolina 69,526), and the attacker demanded payment not to publish. No group has claimed the attack. (Single source.)
- https://therecord.media/irhythm-data-breach-reports

### FBI breach arrest — suspect reportedly co-founded a ransomware negotiation firm
A Canadian man was arrested in Pennsylvania on 8 October on cyber-extortion conspiracy charges, reported as the second arrest over the ShinyHunters theft of an estimated 2–3TB from the FBI's contractor-run recruitment portal, where a missed patch gave entry. Krebs on Security identifies him — not yet officially confirmed — as Edward Dubrovsky, co-founder of ransomware negotiation firm Cypfer.

## Active campaigns

### US and allies disrupt Flax Typhoon tooling tied to Beijing contractor Integrity Technology Group
The FBI seized seven domains supporting the China-linked group's MicroScan scanning and FishHub spear-phishing platforms, and agencies from the US, UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand and Spain issued a joint advisory on Integrity Technology Group's role in intrusions at critical infrastructure. CISA added five older flaws the group exploits — CVE-2015-3306 (ProFTPD), CVE-2021-3199 (ONLYOFFICE), CVE-2023-22894 (Strapi), CVE-2016-3081 (Apache Struts), CVE-2015-5477 (ISC BIND) — to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue with an 11 October 2026 deadline for US federal agencies.

### Credential-stealing GitHub Actions workflows planted across tens of thousands of repositories
A revival of the GhostAction supply-chain campaign is using stolen maintainer tokens to push fake "security audit" workflows (security-audit.yml / github_actions_security.yml) that harvest repository secrets and cloud, AI and SaaS API keys, exfiltrating them over plain HTTP. Researchers at Socket count more than 500 compromised accounts and tens of thousands of affected repositories since 7 October 2026; forks of infected repositories can inherit and run the malicious workflow. (Single source.)
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/credential-stealing-github-actions.html
