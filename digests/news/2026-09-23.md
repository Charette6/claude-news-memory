### Microsoft and UK police take down EvilTokens phishing service
Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit, with UK police, Health-ISAC and SpyCloud, dismantled the EvilTokens phishing-as-a-service platform; two suspected administrators were arrested in the UK and released on bail. The service compromised over 12,000 Microsoft accounts across more than 10,000 organisations in 79 countries, with the US, Canada, Australia, the UK and Saudi Arabia the most-targeted countries.

### Joint advisory on North Korean WaterPlum campaign; Japan dismantles laptop farm
Japan, the US, Germany and Australia jointly detailed North Korea's WaterPlum ("Contagious Interview") fake-recruiter campaign: at least 30,000 devices infected in 100+ countries, over 7,000 cryptocurrency wallets compromised and roughly $10.7 million stolen between December 2025 and July 2026. Japan also dismantled its first confirmed North Korean IT-worker laptop farm, and the agencies tied both operations to the 313 General Bureau.

### Chinese group exploiting Zyxel switches, WordPress and other edge devices
A Chinese-speaking group linked to the Red Heron cluster has compromised at least 49 organisations in 29 countries since June 2026 by chaining recently disclosed flaws in Zyxel GS1900 switches (CVE-2026-7273), WordPress and other internet-facing products; 996 compromised switches were counted and more than 18,500 records including plaintext passwords were stolen. Exploitation began within days of public exploit code appearing.

- https://www.securityweek.com/recent-zyxel-switch-vulnerability-exploited-by-chinese-hackers/

### Sweden fines Miljödata over breach affecting 2.2 million (GDPR)
Sweden's data protection authority IMY fined municipal software provider Miljödata SEK 1.8 million (~$183,000) over its August 2025 ransomware breach, which exposed personal data of 2.2 million people. The regulator found violations of GDPR Article 32(1), citing missing software verification procedures and the lack of real-time automated intrusion detection. (single source)
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sweden-fines-milj-data-183-000-over-breach-affecting-22-million/

### Canadian privacy regulator opens probe of IDScan
The Privacy Commissioner of Canada opened an investigation into identity-verification provider IDScan.net after hackers accessed scans of 153 million drivers' licences in its cloud platform. The probe covers the company's security practices and the adequacy of victim notification under Canadian federal private-sector privacy law. (single source)
- https://therecord.media/canadian-regulator-opens-probe-of-idscan-following-data-breach

### ShinyHunters claims FBI breach via Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day
ShinyHunters claims it exploited an unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day to steal 2-3TB of FBI employee and applicant data and defaced the FBI Jobs website; the FBI confirmed only that it is investigating unauthorized activity affecting FBIjobs.gov, and Oracle has not commented. The group says it is now using the same vulnerability against Fortune 500 companies; the data-theft claim remains unverified.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/shinyhunters-claims-fbi-hack-data-theft-in-peoplesoft-zero-day-breach/

### ClosedQuorum AI-directed Windows malware
Cisco Talos documented ClosedQuorum, Go-based Windows malware that queries multiple commercial AI models (Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral) to autonomously choose post-compromise actions including credential theft and lateral movement. No confirmed in-the-wild deployment; it is unclear whether it is operational or experimental.
