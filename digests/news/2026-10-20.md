## Financial sector

### Conduent agrees to pay $80m to settle data breach class action
Conduent, a business-services firm handling government payment disbursements, document processing and back-office support, agreed to an $80m class-action settlement over an October 2024 intrusion attributed to the SafePay ransomware gang that exposed personal and health data of around 62 million people. The attack went undiscovered for almost three months; the settlement was filed in the District of New Jersey and disclosed to the SEC on 16 October 2026, and commits Conduent to strengthen its data security practices.
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/conduent-settle-data-breach-action/

## Regulatory & supervisory

### US DHS orders DeepSeek removed from all federal civilian agencies
The US Department of Homeland Security directed federal civilian agencies to stop using and remove DeepSeek technology, covering all DeepSeek and DeepSeek-affiliate products and third-party products developed using DeepSeek implementations. The order's reach is unclear for AI gateways such as OpenRouter and Vercel AI Gateway, which can route requests to DeepSeek models without the user explicitly choosing them.
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/20/deepseek-deep-sixed-at-all-us-federal-civilian-agencies/5304036

## Major incidents

### ConnectWise suspends cloud services after supply-chain attack on Microsoft 365 tenants
ConnectWise shut down its Cloud Backup, SaaS Security and Cloud Management services after a stolen Cloud Backup credential — which carries broad read access to customer mail, files, Teams and directory data — was used against Microsoft 365 tenants, with unauthorized sign-ins seen in 162 tenants and attackers assigning themselves directory or administrative roles on about 50 systems monitored by Huntress. ConnectWise revoked affected certificates and credentials and says it has seen no successful threat activity since, but cannot tell which customer environments were breached; no restoration timeline has been given.

### Ahold Delhaize confirms cyberattack forced US and European systems offline
Ahold Delhaize confirmed that malicious actors caused the suspicious activity detected in its IT systems last week, forcing it to take systems offline across most of its US and European brands and disrupting online shopping in the US, the Netherlands and Belgium; stores stayed open. The company says it has no evidence so far that customer, employee or partner data was stolen, filed a Dutch police report, and has not described the nature of the attack.
- https://therecord.media/ahold-delhaize-cyberattack-grocery
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/19/grocery-giant-ahold-delhaize-confirms-cyberattack-forced-us-and-european-systems-offline/5303970

### Nikkei discloses third data breach in a month, 1.83 million records exposed
Nikkei said attackers used stolen credentials to access a development system on 7 October, exposing 1.83 million personal-information records stored in the cloud, of which 150,000 contained identifiers such as names, email addresses, phone numbers or dates of birth; no credit card data was involved. It is Nikkei's third disclosed breach in about a month, following compromises of an employee Google Workspace account and a Microsoft 365 account; the Personal Information Protection Commission was notified on 11 October.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/nikkei-discloses-third-data-breach-in-a-month-18m-records-exposed/

## Campaigns

### OpenCode Console breach exposes stored OpenAI, Anthropic and Google API keys
OpenCode said attackers ran a large-scale, AI-agent-driven "distillation attack" on its Console for at least 48 hours, affecting 1.4% of Console users who had linked external providers and exposing stored API keys for Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. Some users reported unauthorized API charges — one over $1,700 overnight — and Google suspended abused cloud projects; affected users have been notified by email.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/opencode-hack-exposes-api-keys-amid-ai-distillation-attacks/
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/opencode-console-breach-may-have.html
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/opencode-breach-openai-anthropic/

### Rank Math PRO WordPress plugin compromised in supply-chain attack
Official downloads of the Rank Math PRO SEO plugin (version 3.0.123) were trojanized for roughly 11 hours between 15 October 20:02 UTC and 16 October 07:12 UTC, planting a backdoor that persists, recreates itself when the infected site is visited, and can expose database credentials, API keys and other secrets. The vendor released a clean version 3.0.124 and warns that deactivating or updating the plugin does not remove the implant; the free version, active on more than 4 million sites, was not affected.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/wordpress-seo-plugin-rank-math-compromised-to-backdoor-websites/
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/popular-wordpress-seo-plugin-rank-math.html
- https://www.securityweek.com/rank-math-pro-wordpress-plugin-compromised-in-supply-chain-attack/

### FBI arrests ransom negotiator suspected of running ShinyHunters extortion site
The FBI arrested Edward Dubrovsky, a Canadian executive who worked at ransomware-negotiation firms, on charges of threatening to disclose stolen data and extortion-related interference with interstate commerce, and investigators suspect an executive at a second, unnamed US consulting firm helped run the ShinyHunters extortion site shinywire[.]com, which listed nearly 100 victims between May and October 2026. The FBI says the group has extorted more than $70m this year; its reported leader, known as "Rey", was detained in Jordan and is said to be cooperating with the FBI.
- https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/feds-suspect-ransom-negotiator-ran-shinyhunters-extortion-site/
