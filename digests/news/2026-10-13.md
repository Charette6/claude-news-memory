# Security News Digest — 2026-10-13

Public record of security news items from the 24 hours to 2026-10-13 08:03 (UTC+3).

## Financial sector

### Ledger confirms spy implant in hardware wallet sold through a reseller
Ledger confirmed an unauthorized hardware implant in a crypto wallet bought through reseller CryptoBilis, after fund-theft reports from buyers in Southeast Asia; a blockchain investigator puts losses above $86m, a figure Ledger has not verified. Ledger says a limited number of devices are affected, all sold through that one reseller, and has paused sales via the channel.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/ledger-finds-unauthorized-hardware.html
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ledger-confirms-hardware-wallet-spy-implant-behind-crypto-thefts/

### Leader of 15,000-mule money-laundering network pleads guilty in the US
Oleg Korniev pleaded guilty to leading Your Mule Cashout, a network that recruited more than 15,000 US residents as unwitting money mules and laundered at least $10m stolen from over 750 US business bank accounts, moving it mainly to Eastern Europe. Four other members were already extradited, convicted and ordered to pay over $9.1m each in restitution.
- https://www.securityweek.com/mastermind-of-15000-mule-cybercrime-laundering-network-pleads-guilty-in-us/ (single source)

## Regulatory & supervisory

### Norway issues record $9.6m GDPR fine to Microsoft ad unit over Grindr data
Norway's Data Protection Authority fined Xandr (now Microsoft Advertising) NOK 92m — its largest fine ever — for unlawfully processing and sharing sensitive Grindr user data through real-time ad auctions between 2018 and 2020. The regulator held Xandr liable as a joint controller for failing to verify that the consents its partner collected were valid.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/norway-fines-microsoft-ad-firm-96-million-over-grindr-data-sharing/ (single source)

## Major incidents elsewhere

### Hackers remotely spoiled food at hundreds of French supermarkets via refrigeration controllers
Starting 11 October, attackers forced defrost cycles — temperatures set as high as 50°C — on internet-exposed, outdated Danfoss refrigeration controllers, hitting roughly 150 Intermarché stores, about 80 Carrefour stores, around 5% of Monoprix supermarkets and several Coopérative U stores. No attribution yet; a Shodan scan found about 3,726 such controllers reachable from the public internet.
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/french-supermarket-chains/
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cyberattack-shuts-down-refrigerators-at-french-supermarkets/

### Grocery giant Ahold Delhaize takes systems offline after cyberattack
Ahold Delhaize — 9,400 stores and roughly 60 million customers weekly across Europe, the US and Indonesia — confirmed a cybersecurity incident on 12 October and took some systems offline as a precaution, disrupting customer websites, apps and employee scheduling at brands including Albert Heijn, Delhaize and Stop & Shop. The company has not said how far the attack reached; it hit the same day as the French refrigeration attacks, but no link is confirmed.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/retail-giant-ahold-delhaize-takes-systems-offline-after-cyberattack/ (single source)

### Drone strikes on Yandex data centers knock Russian cloud and payment services offline
Ukrainian drones have struck three of Yandex's six Russian data centers in the past week, shutting the latest one down completely and disrupting Yandex Cloud, email and Yandex Pay payments. Yandex has told its roughly 64,000 cloud customers to plan recovery beyond its own infrastructure and has given no restoration timeline.
- https://therecord.media/ukraine-strikes-third-yandex-data-center
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/12/ukrainian-drone-strikes-on-yandex-datacenters-knock-cloud-and-payments-offline/5302577
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/yandex-confirms-service-outage-as-users-lose-access-to-cloud-files/

## Active campaigns

### Criminal access broker ran 51 AI agents to automate intrusions at machine speed
Researchers at SOCRadar exposed CyberXero, a Russian-speaking access broker using a cluster of 51 AI coding agents plus open-source pentest tooling to automate exploitation of WordPress and e-commerce sites and of Ukrainian infrastructure; one automated run scanned 4,708 targets and planted 32 web shells in 61 seconds. An estimated 628,000 records on Ukrainian individuals were exfiltrated, and the campaign is described as ongoing.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cyberxero-uses-claude-code-in-campaign.html
- https://securityaffairs.com/200803/cyber-crime/cyberxero-used-51-ai-agents-to-steal-628000-ukrainian-records.html

### Prominent ransomware negotiator is third arrest in ShinyHunters FBI-breach case
A Canadian executive linked to Toronto ransomware-negotiation firm CyberSteward — reported to be Edward Dubrovsky — was arrested in Philadelphia on extortion-related charges tied to the ShinyHunters breach of FBI systems, which exposed data on nearly all agents and applicants. It is the third arrest in weeks, after suspects were detained in the Netherlands and in Jordan, the latter reportedly cooperating with the FBI.

- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/net-closes-shinyhunters-another/
