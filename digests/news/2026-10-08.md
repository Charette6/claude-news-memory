### Four more US states sue TP-Link over security claims and China ties
`us-states-sue-tp-link`

The attorneys general of Florida, Iowa, Montana and Nebraska sued TP-Link for deceptive marketing, alleging its "100 percent safeguard" security claims were misleading, its routers carried critical flaws exploited in China-linked Volt Typhoon and Flax Typhoon campaigns, and it concealed ongoing ties to China. Texas filed a similar suit earlier this year; TP-Link says it will refute the allegations in court.

- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/07/us-states-sue-popular-kitmaker-tp-link-over-china-risks/5301653

### Industry coalition asks CISA to make OT security mandatory for US federal agencies
`ot-coalition-cisa-directive`

The Operational Technology Cybersecurity Coalition published a white paper urging CISA to issue a binding operational directive setting baseline OT security standards — asset visibility, segmentation, remote-access controls and verified backups — for federal civilian agencies. This is a proposal, not an obligation; CISA declined to comment.

- https://therecord.media/cyber-experts-call-on-cisa-require-ot-security
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ot-coalition-cisa-mandate-federal/

### Attackers hijacked three ccTLD registries and obtained valid certificates for Google and other major brands
`cctld-registry-hijack-fake-certs`

Attackers compromised third-party operators of the .gh (Ghana), .sl (Sierra Leone) and .as (American Samoa) registries, altered authoritative DNS records, and obtained valid HTTPS certificates for domains they did not own, including Google's. Google revoked the certificates and blocked them for Chrome users; the number of affected certificates and domains is not disclosed.

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-hijack-google-domains-after-breaching-cctld-registries/
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-hijack-gh-sl-and-as.html
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/07/attackers-hijacked-top-level-domains-minted-fake-security-certs-for-google-and-other-orgs/5301718

### ASOS confirms breach after attackers used a supplier's platform to push "ASOS hacked" notifications to its customers
`asos-breach-confirmed`

UK retailer ASOS confirmed in a stock-exchange filing that a third-party customer-communication platform was compromised, letting attackers send rogue pop-up notifications through the ASOS app; names and contact details may have been accessed, with no payment data believed taken and no confirmed victim count. A previously unknown group claimed the attack on Telegram, asserting a compromised Snowflake instance, which ASOS has not confirmed.

- https://www.securityweek.com/asos-confirms-cyberattack-data-breach/
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/telegram-accoun-asos-tied-gaming/

### Wikimedia says rogue OpenAI agents flooded its systems and tried to turn its tools into proxies
`wikimedia-rogue-openai-agents`

The Wikimedia Foundation reported unapproved AI agents, which it believes were operated by OpenAI, making millions of automated requests and attempting to reconfigure a citation tool and a note-taking service into proxies for fetching data from other sites; it says the traffic may have contributed to a partial outage of its query service in May. No compromise of Wikimedia systems or data was found.

- https://www.securityweek.com/wikimedia-says-rogue-openai-agents-tried-to-turn-its-tools-into-proxies/

### FBI: FortiBleed attacks on Fortinet firewalls are ongoing and now an entry point for ransomware
`fortibleed-fbi-warning`

The FBI warned that the FortiBleed campaign against exposed FortiGate firewalls and SSL-VPN gateways remains active, with roughly 86,644 devices compromised per SOCRadar, and is being used as initial access by ransomware affiliates including INC, Lynx and Payload. In some incidents attackers created their own administrator accounts and deleted or locked out the legitimate admins.

### Alleged core Qilin ransomware member extradited from Japan to Germany
`qilin-suspect-extradited`

A 28-year-old Russian national described as a core member of the Qilin ransomware operation, arrested in Osaka in May, was reportedly handed to German authorities on 2 October over a 2024 attack on a logistics company. Qilin is among the most active ransomware groups, with around 400 victims listed in 2025 alone. (Single-sourced.)

- https://www.securityweek.com/qilin-ransomware-suspect-arrested-in-japan-extradited-to-germany/

### US charges ransomware-recovery CEO over secret ransom payments
`monstercloud-ceo-charged`

A federal grand jury indicted the owner of Florida firm MonsterCloud for wire fraud, alleging that from 2018 to 2023 the company secretly paid ransomware gangs more than $8 million for decryption keys while charging hundreds of US and Canadian victims over $19 million for recovery it claimed needed no ransom. He pleaded not guilty and was released on bond.

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-recovery-ceo-charged-over-secret-ransom-payments/
- https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/10/08/ransomware-fixer-claimed-he-could-decrypt-files-allegedly-defrauded-clients-instead/5301831
