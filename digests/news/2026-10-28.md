# Security News Digest — 2026-10-28

Window: 24 hours to 2026-10-28 08:03 (UTC+3). 12 items.

## Stories

### Japanese hosting firm GMO tells customers to unplug PCs amid ransomware extortion
An attacker breached Onamae Desktop Cloud, a GMO Internet VPS service widely used to host MetaTrader 4/5 trading terminals, with up to 26,739 customer servers potentially affected, and published stolen company and customer data after GMO refused an ¥8bn (~$50m) ransom. GMO warned MT4/MT5 credentials and trading-account details may be compromised and asked customers to back up, disconnect and shut down their machines.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japans-gmo-urges-customers-to-shut-down-pcs-amid-ransomware-threat/ (single source)

### Card skimmer planted in shared analytics script of Brazilian checkout platform Convertr
Sansec found attackers modified a self-hosted analytics script served by Convertr, a platform handling about $370m in sales for dozens of fashion brands, skimming card number, expiry, CVV, cardholder name and taxpayer ID at checkout across all merchants on the platform. The skimmer ran from 24 October until Convertr removed it; no victim count has been confirmed.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-hijack-convertr-analytics-script-to-steal-payment-card-data/ (single source)

### Hardware wallet maker Keystone to destroy 18,835 devices after device-authentication key theft
An attacker who breached Keystone's website stole the key its crypto hardware wallets use to prove authenticity, along with 25,964 buyer records and 12,873 incomplete-checkout records; recovery phrases, private keys and payment data were not involved. Keystone is destroying 18,835 unsold devices carrying the old key and has paused website shipping.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/keystone-to-destroy-18835-hardware.html (single source)

### UK watchdog: suppliers holding police data repeatedly breached
HMICFRS reported that third-party suppliers holding UK police data have been repeatedly compromised since 2021, with 252 cyber incidents and 796 near misses recorded in one quarter. It found most supplier contracts had no ongoing cybersecurity assurance, incident-reporting duties were unclear, and supplier logs were often invisible to in-house teams.
- https://therecord.media/uk-police-data-breached-from-suppliers-watchdog-report
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/watchdog-police-fire-service/

### Ransomware at University of New Mexico Health forced ambulance diversions and cancelled surgeries
A cyberattack on UNM's Health Sciences Center forced ambulance diversions, suspended outpatient surgeries and cancelled classes on 26–27 October; a new ransomware group calling itself Byod claims 36,000 systems including Active Directory, the Entra tenant and patient records — claims UNM has not confirmed. UNM says critical systems are back online and hospitals fully open.

### University of Copenhagen breach exposes data on 525,000 people
Attackers accessed a test environment holding a real database of about 525,000 people's names, national identity numbers, phone numbers and email addresses; physical-access PIN codes for about 20,000 staff and students were also compromised. A second attack on 26 October forced the university to shut down administrative systems; the case has been reported to the Danish Data Protection Agency under GDPR.
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/copenhagen-university-data-breach/
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/university-of-copenhagen-data-breach-exposes-data-of-525-000-people/

### Energy giant ENGIE discloses customer data breach via partner marketing platform
ENGIE confirmed attackers stole customer contact details, meter identifiers and contract numbers from a marketing and prospecting tool used by its business partners; it says its own IT systems were not touched, no banking data was taken, and it has not given a victim count. Affected customers in France are being notified individually.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/energy-giant-engie-discloses-data-breach-affecting-customers/ (single source)

### Fake IT support over Microsoft Teams external chat: malware running in under 15 minutes
Arctic Wolf documented over 400 incidents in which attackers mail-bomb an employee's inbox, then message them via Teams external chat (on by default) posing as "IT Service Desk", talk them into Quick Assist, AnyDesk or ScreenConnect, and execute a PowerShell payload — in some cases within 15 minutes, sometimes leading to ransomware. Ransomware groups including Black Basta have used this vector.
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/exploit-teams-chat-breach/
- https://www.darkreading.com/cloud-security/email-bombing-external-teams-chats-repeatable-access-path
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/27/microsoft-teams-social-engineering-fake-it-help-desk/

### Lazarus abuses mandatory South Korean banking security software in espionage campaign
Kaspersky says North Korea's Lazarus Group is exploiting INISAFE CrossWeb EX — a security client South Korean users must install for online banking and government portals — to sideload malware, in a campaign ("Operation FlashHole") running since at least July 2025 with at least three confirmed South Korean victims. No financial institutions are named as victims so far.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/lazarus-abuses-south-korean-security.html (single source)
