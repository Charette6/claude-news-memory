## Stories

### Citrix confirms two NetScaler zero-days exploited in the wild
Citrix confirmed that CVE-2026-88771 (unauthenticated RCE, CVSS 9.5) and CVE-2026-88772 (memory overflow requiring DTLS, CVSS 9.5) in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway are being actively exploited against unmitigated deployments, with multiple customer environments compromised worldwide per the Dutch NCSC. Fixed builds are available (14.1-73.37, 13.1-64.23 and FIPS variants); CISA issued an alert and added the flaws to the KEV catalog. Vulnerability details are covered in the Vulnerability Brief.

### SharePoint RCE CVE-2026-65660 now exploited in attacks
A SharePoint code-injection RCE patched in Microsoft's August 2026 updates is now being exploited, with attackers attempting to plant webshells; CISA added it to the KEV catalog on September 25 with a September 28 federal patching deadline. Initially misclassified as medium-severity spoofing, it was reclassified as high-severity RCE. Single-sourced in this window; covered in the Vulnerability Brief.
- https://www.securityweek.com/microsoft-sharepoint-flaw-cve-2026-65660-now-exploited-in-attacks/

### Cloudflare fixes Containers cross-tenant data exposure flaw
Cloudflare fixed a flaw in its Containers and Sandboxes service that let customers on shared hosts read residual disk data from other tenants, including SQLite databases, .env and credential files, caused by a storage pool skipping the zeroing of reused blocks. The researcher's testing found residual material on 18 of 24 container placements; Cloudflare completed mitigations by September 19 and reports no evidence any customer data was actually exposed. Single-sourced.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cloudflare-fixes-containers-cross-tenant-flaw-exposing-customer-data/

### Admin of Rydox stolen-data marketplace pleads guilty in the US
Ardit Kutleshi, a Kosovo national, pleaded guilty to running Rydox, a marketplace operating since 2016 that sold stolen personal data, access devices and cybercrime tools; his brother was convicted earlier. Prosecutors cite roughly 18,000 users, over 321,000 products listed and over 7,600 transactions, with sentencing set for February 9, 2027. Single-sourced.
- https://securityaffairs.com/199825/uncategorized/rydox-admin-faces-20-years-after-selling-stolen-data-and-fraud-tools.html
