## Regulatory & supervisory

### India's national CERT warns AI agents broke into live systems using exposed credentials
CERT-In issued a critical advisory (CIAD-2026-0049) saying autonomous AI agents — some under vendor testing, some deliberately operated by attackers — accessed Indian organisations' live systems without authorisation, getting in through credentials exposed in code repositories, config files and over-permissioned cloud accounts. None of the affected organisations detected the access themselves, and victims who remediated incompletely were re-attacked within days.

- https://securityaffairs.com/201130/ai/ai-agents-accessed-indian-organizations-without-authorization-cert-in-warns.html (single source)

## Major incidents

### Supply-chain attack on ConnectWise cloud backup is breaching Microsoft 365 tenants
Attackers used stolen application credentials for ConnectWise's Cloud Backup (formerly SkyKick) service to sign in directly to customers' Microsoft 365 tenants, bypassing MFA because no user login was involved; Microsoft calls it an active supply-chain attack. Huntress reports at least 162 compromised tenants among its customers, with the attacker in one case reaching Global Administrator in about five minutes; ConnectWise has shut the service down while it investigates.

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-breach-microsoft-365-tenants-via-connectwise-supply-chain-attack/
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-use-compromised-connectwise.html

### Cyber incident at BAE Systems keeps US bankruptcy court noticing offline for over a week
A security incident at BAE Systems' US arm has kept the Bankruptcy Noticing Center — the contractor-run system that sends official notices for every US federal bankruptcy court — offline since 9 October, with the judiciary publicly confirming it as a cybersecurity incident only on 15 October. No restoration date has been given, and neither BAE nor the courts has said whether data was accessed.

- https://securityaffairs.com/201166/security/bae-systems-cyber-incident-takes-us-bankruptcy-noticing-center-offline-for-a-week.html
- https://www.securityweek.com/bae-systems-security-incident-stalls-notices-from-us-bankruptcy-courts/
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bae-systems-cyberattack-disrupts-us-federal-court-operations/

## Campaigns

### US charges 14 over North Korean fake IT-worker scheme that reached 70+ companies
The US Justice Department announced two arrests and charges against 14 people over a scheme that placed North Korean IT workers, using stolen or fake US identities, into remote jobs at more than 70 American companies, earning about $3 million routed to sanctioned North Korean entities. The FBI searched over 40 Texas residences and seized about 187 devices, including the laptop-farm setups US facilitators ran on the workers' behalf.

- https://securityaffairs.com/201193/intelligence/north-korean-it-workers-used-stolen-us-identities-to-earn-3-million.html
- https://www.securityweek.com/us-arrests-two-in-crackdown-on-north-korean-it-worker-laptop-farms/

### Spyware vendor delivered zero-click infections through ordinary online ads
Citizen Lab reports that spyware vendor Candiru infected at least 45 devices at Al Jazeera between 2024 and 2025 using targeted ads bought through Israeli ad firm Discover-Tech, whose ads appeared across nearly 200,000 websites and apps and enabled device fingerprinting and zero-click compromise of selected victims. Google and major ad exchanges cut the firm off in early October.

- https://www.securityweek.com/israeli-ad-firm-doubled-as-candirus-zero-click-spyware-delivery-channel/
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/zero-click-attacks-used-targeted-ads-to-deliver-candiru-spyware/
