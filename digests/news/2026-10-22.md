# Security News Digest — 2026-10-22

Window: 24 hours to 2026-10-22 08:03 (UTC+3). Public record of security news; neutral summaries and sources only.

## Financial sector

### State Bank of India confirms customer data leak found on the dark web
SBI told stock exchanges it experienced a data-loss incident after CERT-In and the Defense Cyber Agency alerted it on October 16 to a dark-web listing claiming roughly 375,000 rows; the bank has verified 3,897 records so far, containing names, contact details, dates of birth and branch/CIF identifiers, but no account, card or credential data. The cause is under investigation by an external forensic firm from CERT-In's panel, and the bank says core operations and finances are unaffected. (Single source.)
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/state-bank-of-india-confirms-data-leak.html

## Regulatory & supervisory

### US court orders Meta to pay $25 billion over Cambridge Analytica privacy violations
A New Mexico judge set the penalty at $25 billion (reduced from an initial $220 billion award) after a September jury verdict that Meta violated the state's unfair practices law 43 million times over data shared with Cambridge Analytica; 15% annual interest accrues until paid. Meta is appealing. (Single source.)
- https://therecord.media/meta-new-mexico-penalty-cambridge-analytica-privacy-case

### EU opens Digital Services Act investigation into Booking.com over customer fraud
The European Commission is investigating whether Booking.com, as a very large online platform under the DSA, does enough to prevent fraudulent listings, phishing, smishing and account takeovers targeting its users, and whether it adequately separates ads from organic results. No deadline or penalty was announced; Booking.com says it is cooperating fully. (Single source.)
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/21/eu-wants-bookingcom-to-check-out-of-its-scam-problem/5304413

## Major incidents

### UPDATE: Browsers distrust eMudhra CA certificates after recoverable private keys
Chrome, Firefox, Apple and Microsoft blocked eight eMudhra intermediate certificates whose private keys were trivially recoverable (integer values under 256), and Mozilla removed trust in the signing root, with Google evaluating eMudhra's remaining CA certificate. eMudhra revoked the certificates on October 18 and estimates about 30,000 customer certificates need replacing; systems that do not receive revocation updates may continue to trust the certificates.
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/21/emudhra-certificates-private-keys-compromised/
- https://www.securityweek.com/browsers-block-emudhra-intermediate-cas-over-recoverable-private-keys/
- https://blog.talosintelligence.com/compromised-intermediate-certificates/
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/mozilla-google-apple-block-compromised-emudhra-ca-certificates/

### OVHcloud's official CLI tool briefly replaced with a credential stealer
An attacker who had controlled an OVHcloud developer's GitHub account since March 2026 overwrote published releases of the OVHcloud CLI (versions 0.11.0–0.15.0) with a secret-stealing binary on October 19; it was live for about 94 minutes before a user's hash check flagged it. OVHcloud says CI/CD pipelines were unaffected — GitHub release immutability was not enabled at the time and has since been turned on; researchers linked a related loader to GlassWorm indicators.
- https://www.securityweek.com/hijacked-developer-account-used-to-plant-stealer-in-ovhcloud-cli/
- https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/ovhcloud-github-compromise-glassworm-threat-actors

### Japanese VPS service Onamae Desktop Cloud breached; up to 27,000 customer servers affected
GMO Internet found malware installed on customer servers of its Onamae Desktop Cloud VPS service (marketed for automated MetaTrader FX trading) and shut the platform down at 04:00 local time on October 21, affecting up to 26,739 servers with no restoration date given. The company says payment card data is held on separate infrastructure and was not affected, and advised customers to rotate any credentials stored on their servers. (Single source.)
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japans-onamae-desktop-cloud-hacked-up-to-27-000-servers-affected/

## Campaigns

### AI gateway LiteLLM backdoored on PyPI and Docker Hub
Attackers used stolen GitHub CLI OAuth tokens from three LiteLLM engineers to publish a trojanised version 1.107.0 containing the "privcollect" credential stealer (cloud tokens, keys, browser data, AI tool and proxy credentials) and an hourly backdoor executing attacker-served code. PyPI quarantined the package, the malicious Docker images were removed, and users of 1.107.0 are advised to rotate all credentials from a clean environment and pin 1.106.2 by digest.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/litellm-compromised-in-supply-chain.html
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/21/litellm-supply-chain-attack-hoovers-up-passwords-and-secrets/5304548
