# Security News Digest — 2026-10-02

Public record of security news published in the 24 hours to 2026-10-02 08:00 (UTC+3).

## Financial sector

### Bitget confirms $387.5m theft via zero-day in a third-party security product
Bitget confirmed attackers exploited a zero-day in third-party security products to obtain high-level internal credentials, then issued fraudulent withdrawals of $387.5 million from hot and warm wallets on 25 September 2026. Investigators (Mandiant and SlowMist) traced malicious activity back to 31 August, attribute the operation to North Korean actors based on IP and on-chain analysis, and about $1.1 million has been frozen so far.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/bitget-confirms-third-party-zero-day.html

### MetaMask discloses infrastructure incident, pulls its staking validators
MetaMask disclosed an ongoing security incident affecting part of its infrastructure on 1 October, saying there is no immediate threat to user wallets but declining to say what was accessed. As a precaution it is exiting validators from its non-custodial staking operations, expected to complete by 7 October; no scale figures were given.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/metamask-discloses-security-incident-affecting-its-infrastructure/
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/metamask-security-incident-prompts-exit.html

### Polish invoicing platform Fakturownia breached; bank account data among what was exposed
Fakturownia, an invoicing service used by over 600,000 businesses, confirmed unauthorised access exposing account data, password hashes, bank account information, authentication and integration tokens, and pre-2023 invoices; it is still determining how many customers are affected. The breach was reported to Polish data protection and cybersecurity authorities, and an attacker's claim of 6 terabytes stolen remains unverified.
- https://therecord.media/poland-cyberattack-invoice-software

## Regulatory & supervisory

### US Treasury sanctions the network behind Ploutus ATM jackpotting malware
The US Treasury blacklisted the alleged developer of the Ploutus ATM malware, seven associates and two Mexico-based companies, tied to more than $40 million in losses across more than 1,500 jackpotting attacks; 119 people have been indicted in the wider conspiracy. Proceeds were laundered through cryptocurrency and distributed to the Tren de Aragua network, and foreign financial institutions face secondary sanctions risk for significant transactions with the designees.
- https://www.securityweek.com/treasury-blacklists-most-wanted-atm-malware-developer-and-his-network/

## Major incidents elsewhere

### US defence HR system breach exposed records of over 3 million people
The Pentagon confirmed attackers exploited a vulnerability in Defense Manpower Data Center file-sharing systems and had access between October 2025 and July 2026, exposing Social Security numbers and personal data of nearly 2.8 million living and 294,000 deceased individuals. Affected people are being offered 12 months of credit monitoring.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-breach-pentagon-human-resources-management-system-steal-data-of-nearly-3-million-people/

### AI-driven attack breached the Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure in seconds
DIVD confirmed an agentic AI-powered attack chained two zero-days in its Zammad helpdesk software (unauthenticated remote code execution and escalation to root, both CVSS 9.4) on 21 September, pivoted to other services and exfiltrated data, with exploitation taking seconds; network segmentation contained the damage. It is one of the first well-documented intrusions run end-to-end by autonomous AI agents.
- https://www.securityweek.com/zammad-zero-days-exploited-in-ai-powered-divd-hack/
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/01/divd-agentic-ai-attack-breach/
- https://securityaffairs.com/200126/hacking/ai-agent-chains-zammad-zero-days-to-take-over-divd-systems-in-seconds.html
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/01/ai-agents-hacked-the-hackers-stealing-email-addresses-from-security-research-org/5300652

## Active campaigns

### KillSec ransomware dismantled; suspected 16-year-old administrator arrested
Operation KillSwitch, led by German authorities with police in ten countries plus Europol and Eurojust, shut down KillSec's servers and dark-web leak site, recovered at least 110 terabytes of stolen data, and provisionally arrested three suspects including a 16-year-old alleged main administrator. Europol links the group to around 1,000 suspected attacks worldwide, roughly 500 of them confirmed.

### China-linked phishing impersonates AI figures to steal Microsoft 365 logins
Proofpoint reports that China-aligned group TA419 impersonated a former White House science-policy official, a prominent economist and an Anthropic employee in credential-phishing against AI policy experts, using dozens of spoofed domains and fake OneDrive pages with attacker-in-the-middle harvesting of Microsoft 365 logins. Activity ran mainly in July 2026, with earlier waves in February.
