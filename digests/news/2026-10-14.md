## Stories

### Tampered Ledger hardware wallets tied to an estimated $86 million in crypto thefts
Ledger confirmed that hardware signing devices sold through Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis contained a physical implant that captured users' recovery phrases during setup and transmitted them to attackers over LTE, and said on 13 October that every confirmed loss traces to devices from that reseller. On-chain investigators estimate thefts may exceed $86 million; Ledger has not confirmed a total or a victim count.
- https://www.securityweek.com/86-million-crypto-heist-linked-to-ledger-signer-hardware-implant/
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ledger-confirms-hardware-wallet-spy-implant-behind-crypto-thefts/

### Norway issues record €8.6M GDPR fine to Microsoft-owned adtech firm Xandr over Grindr data
Norway's data protection authority fined Xandr €8.6 million, its largest penalty ever, for relaying Grindr users' data revealing sexual orientation to potentially thousands of advertising companies between 2018 and 2020. The regulator held Xandr liable as a joint controller for relying on Grindr's consent instead of establishing its own legal basis; Microsoft is reviewing the decision.

### UK plans mandatory reporting of serious AI security incidents
The UK government announced amendments to its Cyber Security and Resilience Bill to address AI "loss-of-control" risks, including required identification and reporting of serious AI incidents and near misses across essential services, plus NCSC-led work on responding to incidents involving AI agents. Timelines are vague and a National Cyber Action Plan is promised "soon". (Single source.)
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/uk-government-sweeping-ai-security/

### Attackers remotely heated fridges at hundreds of French supermarkets via maintenance access
Overnight on 11 October, attackers used remotely managed Danfoss refrigeration controllers, mostly end-of-life models, to put fridges and freezers into defrost at several hundred stores across Intermarché, Carrefour, Coopérative U, Monoprix and others, forcing stock to be destroyed; one retail group's CEO expects losses in the tens of millions of euros (an expectation, not a confirmed figure). CERT-FR told operators to disconnect or strictly filter access to the controllers, Paris prosecutors opened an inquiry, and no attacker or entry method has been confirmed.

### Three more European retailers confirm separate cyber incidents in the same week
Grocery group Ahold Delhaize took systems offline after detecting a security issue, disrupting deliveries and online orders at Albert Heijn, Etos and Gall & Gall, while The Foschini Group confirmed an incident affecting its UK brands Phase Eight, Hobbs and Whistles, and H&M is examining claims of a potential incident it has not substantiated. No data theft is confirmed at Ahold Delhaize, and reporting indicates no established link between any of these incidents or the French refrigeration attack.

### Denmark: national ID register data of 8.8 million accessed via supplier using password "123456"
Denmark confirmed that data on 8.8 million people in its Central Register of Persons was accessed through Pays ApS, a small IT firm with authorised registry access, after an attacker used the password "123456" on at least three of its accounts, including an administrator account, and ran around 14 million automated searches over ten days in September. The intrusion was detected on 2 October only when a staffer noticed an unusually high-activity invoice. (Single source.)
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/13/123456-password-linked-to-denmark-data-breach-affecting-88m-people/5302726

### Ransomware extortion increasingly includes physical threats against executives and families
A Semperis survey of 1,200 organisations hit by ransomware found 37% of attacks involved psychological or physical threats against staff, with roughly a quarter of victims doxxed, 23% hit by swatting calls, and incident responders describing executives receiving photos of their children's schools. Over half of respondents said attackers carried through on threats in the past year.
- https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/10/14/ransomware-gets-personal-we-know-where-you-are-kind-of-stuff/5302939
- https://www.darkreading.com/cybersecurity-analytics/ransomware-gangs-physical-threats-family-stalking

### US arrests two over laptop farms placing North Korean IT workers inside ~70 companies
The US DOJ arrested two US nationals and indicted several North Korean and Chinese nationals over laptop farms that let North Korean IT workers, hired remotely under stolen identities, work inside about 70 US companies from 2019 to 2026, earning over $3 million for Pyongyang. Raids in September covered more than 40 residences and seized 187 laptops. (Single source.)
- https://therecord.media/north-korea-laptop-farms-worker-scams-doj-raids-texas

### Anthropic research: small AI models could self-replicate across consumer IoT devices
Anthropic published research projecting that small AI models could become capable of self-replicating across cheap consumer IoT devices within months; the experiments ran under simplified conditions (including unauthenticated ADB access) and no autonomous self-replicating system was demonstrated on real hardware.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/anthropic-warns-self-replicating-ai-could-spread-to-your-smart-devices/
