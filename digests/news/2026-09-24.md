## Stories

### New Android banking malware targets users in Europe, Canada and the Middle East
Group-IB documented RemControl, a malware-as-a-service Android trojan distributed through fake Google Play pages impersonating the TVTap IPTV app; it carries more than 30 overlay screens that steal banking credentials, PINs and card details and gives operators real-time remote control of infected devices. Confirmed targeting covers users in Italy, France, Spain, Poland, Portugal, Canada and Middle Eastern countries. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-remcontrol-android-banking-malware-targets-users-in-europe-and-canada/

### Automated AI attack campaign stole 600,000 payment cards from online retailers
Researchers at Gambit report a financially motivated actor using autonomous AI agent frameworks (Strix, Cairn, Hermes) to attack hundreds of online retailers since July, stealing more than 600,000 valid card records from two companies and planting skimmers on five others' sites. In one five-day stretch in September the operation launched over 100 attack waves and compromised at least 27 companies. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/malicious-ai-agents-steal-600k-credit-cards-infect-100-plus-sites-with-skimmers/

### EU auditors say cyber incident information sharing between member states still doesn't work
The EU Court of Auditors found that threat and incident information exchange between national CSIRTs and EU-level bodies is hampered by undefined roles, missing cooperation agreements and slow NIS2 transposition, despite a €1.4bn cybersecurity budget. It recommends the EU adopt CISA-style automated indicator sharing. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/eu-auditors-slam-blocs-cyberinfo/

### ShinyHunters claims breach of FBI jobs portal, backs it with verified samples
The extortion group defaced FBIjobs.gov and claims to have stolen data on FBI agents and job applicants, reportedly via an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day; news outlets verified a 5,000-record sample as legitimate and the FBI confirmed it is investigating. The full scope is not confirmed, and the group is demanding the FBI retract an earlier advisory about it.

### Remote-access gateways under active attack: F5 BIG-IP, Check Point and Arista flaws added to CISA KEV
CISA added four actively exploited flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog — an unauthenticated RCE zero-day in F5 BIG-IP APM (CVE-2026-94127), two Check Point flaws (CVE-2026-85102, CVE-2026-93616) with known customer victims, and an Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator zero-day (CVE-2026-93952) — with a September 25 remediation deadline for US federal agencies. CVE detail is covered in the Daily Vulnerability Brief.

### Microsoft-led coalition dismantles AI-powered phishing service EvilTokens; operator arrested
Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit and partners took down EvilTokens, a phishing-as-a-service platform that abused device-code authentication and used AI to identify payment decision-makers inside more than 12,000 compromised inboxes across over 10,000 organisations; London's Metropolitan Police arrested its operator on September 11. Customers used it to impersonate finance staff and vendors to redirect payments.
- https://securityaffairs.com/199593/cyber-crime/eviltokens-made-phishing-as-a-service-look-easy-then-it-got-taken-down.html
- https://www.securityweek.com/ai-powered-phishing-platform-eviltokens-disrupted-by-microsoft/

### GitLab issue email addresses embed a reusable account token
Aikido researchers showed that a leaked GitLab issue email address contains a non-expiring token that lets anyone push code and run CI jobs as the account owner, bypassing IP restrictions and 2FA; GitLab considers the behaviour intended and has made no functional change yet.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/a-leaked-gitlab-issue-email-address.html

### Ransomware attacks hit a 2026 record in August
NCC Group counted 1,073 ransomware victims in August 2026, up 12% from July and the second consecutive monthly high for the year; industrial was the most-hit sector (31%), with financial services at 6%. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ransomware-attacks-reach-record/

### Ryuk ransomware member sentenced to 24 months
Karen Vardanyan, described by the US DOJ as a core Ryuk member involved in the gang's 2,400+ attacks, was sentenced to two years in federal prison and ordered to pay $1.2m restitution after personally extorting more than $1.2m from victims.
- https://therecord.media/ransomware-ryuk-sentenced-doj
