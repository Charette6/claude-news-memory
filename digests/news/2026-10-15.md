## Financial sector

### Android trojan "Hirudo" replaces banking apps with tampered copies
ThreatFabric uncovered Hirudo, an Android trojan delivered through fake shopping and video apps that uninstalls the victim's real banking app and installs a patched clone with the app's protections (accessibility detection, screenshot blocking, Play Integrity checks) stripped out, then steals passwords, payment PINs and SMS. It impersonates at least seven Indonesian banks and targets financial institutions in China, India, Mexico, Morocco and Thailand; victim numbers are unknown, but researchers found thousands of stolen credential entries on the operators' backend.

- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hirudo-android-trojan-uses-patched.html

### Ledger confirms hardware implant in wallets sold via resellers, halts sales across Asia
Ledger confirmed that hardware wallets sold through a Southeast Asian reseller contained a physical implant that reads the 24-word recovery phrase and exfiltrates it over its own LTE eSIM, invisible to the device's integrity self-checks. All confirmed thefts trace to one reseller, with tampered units also reported in Singapore and at MediaMarkt in Europe; Ledger halted sales in seven Asian markets. No victim or loss totals published. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ledger-asks-sellers-across-asia-to-halt-sales-after-supply-chain-attack/

## Regulatory & supervisory

### UK sanctions crypto payment processor Cryptomus over Russian money laundering
The UK sanctioned Cryptomus (now trading as Heleket) along with VexPay, TokenSpot and Tsunami Payments, freezing UK-held assets and barring British firms from dealing with them; the processor is reported to have served dozens of Russian exchanges and cybercrime services. Figures cited from TRM Labs put more than $7bn through the platform since 2022, and Canada had previously fined it CAD $176m for anti-money-laundering failures. (Single-sourced.)
- https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/10/u-k-sanctions-cryptomus-for-ru-money-laundering/

## Major incidents elsewhere

### TFG London (Phase Eight, Hobbs, Whistles) hit by cyberattack — third UK fashion retailer in a week
TFG London detected an attack on 11 October affecting IT systems at its Phase Eight, Hobbs and Whistles brands; stores remain open, UK authorities are notified, and it is not yet known whether customer data was taken or who is responsible. It follows the ASOS breach days earlier; H&M is investigating so-far-unsubstantiated ShinyHunters claims. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/14/tfg-london-cyberattack-phase-eight-hobbs-whistles/

### French supermarket refrigeration sabotage spreads to a second controller brand
The 11 October attack that remotely changed freezer setpoints at French supermarkets (one store pushed from −22°C to 150°C, settings changing several times a second) now also affects Dixell/Copeland controllers as well as Danfoss, per CERT-FR's advisory; no actor named, no store totals published. Many of the controllers were internet-exposed to support remote temperature monitoring. (Single-sourced in this window.)
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/french-supermarket-hack-more/

## Active campaigns

### Microsoft and Japanese police dismantle phishing platforms behind 750M+ scam messages
A US court order let Microsoft, Japan's National Police Agency and Health-ISAC seize domains behind three Chinese-language platforms (YAM Store, CoGUI, Iron Man) selling ready-made phishing pages impersonating banks, tax agencies and telecoms, plus stolen Microsoft 365 accounts. Microsoft counts more than 750 million messages against its customers since January, impersonation of over 75,000 organisations across 150+ countries, 20,000+ YAM Store-linked accounts disabled and 100,000+ domains being blocked.

### US raids 40 laptop farms behind North Korea's fake remote IT workers; 14 charged
The FBI searched 40 Texas residences suspected of hosting laptop farms for North Korea's remote-IT-worker scheme (Famous Chollima), seized about 187 laptops and KVM devices, arrested two US facilitators and charged 14 people in total, with separate charges against 12 Chinese defendants. One indictment covers fake workers placed at 70 US companies earning roughly $3 million for North Korean actors; some operatives also attempted data theft and malware deployment at employers.

### PEERSTITCH: stealthy implant on FortiGate firewalls after FortiManager compromise at an MSP
Unit 42 describes PEERSTITCH, a memory-only ARM64 implant planted on FortiGate firewalls after attackers (cluster CL-STA-0954 / UNC5820) compromised a telecoms managed service provider's FortiManager via CVE-2024-47575 and used a FortiOS path-traversal flaw to gain root on downstream devices, exfiltrating configurations — including reversibly encrypted admin and VPN credentials — covering more than 500 managed firewalls. The implant leaves no file on disk, opens no ports, and hides its traffic inside legitimate processes; some post-exploitation overlaps with suspected nation-state intrusions. (Single-sourced.)
- https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/fortimanager-cve-2024-47575-peerstitch-malware/
