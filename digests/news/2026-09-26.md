### North Korea blamed for $351m+ theft from crypto exchange Bitget
Attackers breached Bitget's backend wallet systems on 25 September and made unauthorised transfers of ETH, XRP, USDC and other assets; Bitget put the theft at $351.6 million, with early estimates as high as $387.5 million. The CEO attributed the attack to North Korean actors based on IP, behavioural and on-chain evidence, and said a user-protection fund exceeding $464 million will cover all customer losses.

### RemControl Android banking trojan gives attackers remote control of devices
Group-IB detailed RemControl, an Android trojan spread via fake Google Play pages that abuses accessibility permissions to take full remote control of a device, overlay banking apps to harvest credentials, and block its own removal. Since July 2026 it has targeted customers of more than 30 banking institutions across six countries in Western Europe, the Middle East and Canada, run by a Russian-speaking actor who reportedly used an AI assistant to build much of the infrastructure. (Single source.)
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/banking-trojan-remote-control/

### US states fine Labcorp $2.3m over a vendor's breach, impose third-party oversight duties
A coalition of 44 US state attorneys general settled with Labcorp for $2.3 million over the 2019 breach at its debt-collection vendor AMCA, which exposed data on 10.2 million Labcorp customers. Beyond the fine, Labcorp must write cybersecurity requirements into vendor contracts, audit vendor compliance, limit data shared with vendors, and build a dedicated vendor risk-management team. (Single source.)
- https://therecord.media/labcorp-to-overhaul-security-practices-settlement

### Kiteworks tells customers to shut systems down Saturday after government warning
Secure file-transfer vendor Kiteworks (formerly Accellion) urged customers to take systems offline for a six-hour window on Saturday, citing credible threat intelligence from federal intelligence authorities that a threat actor may target customer systems; it says no compromise is confirmed and known vulnerabilities are fixed in version 9.5.1. Its Accellion-era zero-day breach in 2020-21 hit dozens of organisations, including banks.
- https://therecord.media/kiteworks-urges-customers-to-stop-using-systems-incident
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/kiteworks-urges-6-hour-server-shutdown-over-potential-zero-day-attacks/

### Cyberattack on Welsh police force may have exposed staff data
Dyfed-Powys Police, with over 2,000 officers and staff, disclosed a cyberattack discovered earlier in September that disrupted some non-emergency systems. Staff information may have been compromised; the force says it found no evidence that data belonging to the public was affected, and no group has claimed responsibility. (Single source.)
- https://therecord.media/wales-cyberattack-police-breach

### Fake payroll "desktop apps" plant remote-access tool on HR machines
Allure Security found lure pages offering desktop installers for three major web-only US payroll/HR platforms; the installer shows a genuine .NET runtime setup while silently deploying ScreenConnect with unattended access, giving attackers full control of the machine. Researchers describe the goal as "a path to diverting or draining an entire company's payroll"; distribution so far was modest, with 291 downloads of the GitHub-hosted installers.
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/25/fake-payroll-desktop-apps-screenconnect/
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/25/crooks-use-fake-desktop-apps-to-fool-hr-staff-into-giving-them-remote-access/5299226

### ShinyHunters defaces Clop ransomware's data-leak site
The ShinyHunters extortion group exploited an unpatched path-traversal flaw in Grav CMS (confirmed by the CMS maintainers) to deface Clop's data-leak site, claiming to have taken source code, server logs and private keys; Clop disputes that anything of value was there and denies any link between the groups. (Single source.)
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/shinyhunters-hacked-clop-leak-site-using-grav-cms-path-traversal-flaw/

### US soldier sentenced to 70 months for AT&T and Verizon extortion
Cameron John Wagenius ("Kiberphant0m"), 22, was sentenced to 70 months in federal prison plus $294,978 restitution for stealing call and text metadata on over 100 million AT&T customers and extorting the carriers. He and co-conspirators accessed Snowflake cloud accounts protected only by exposed credentials without MFA.
- https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/09/u-s-soldier-gets-70-months-in-prison-for-att-verizon-extortions/

### Roundcube pre-auth SQL injection actively exploited
CVE-2026-48842 (CVSS 8.1), a pre-authentication SQL injection in Roundcube Webmail's virtuser_query plugin (versions 1.6.x before 1.6.16 and 1.7.x before 1.7.1), is being actively exploited and can expose email credentials and stored messages. One-line entry: vulnerability detail is covered by the Vulnerability Brief.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/roundcube-pre-auth-sql-injection-flaw.html

### Compromised GitHub Actions came back online and resumed executing malware
Two GitHub Actions repositories compromised in May 2026 (actions-cool/issues-helper and actions-cool/maintain-one-comment) were re-enabled on 16 September, and workflows referencing them by version tag resumed downloading a payload that harvests CI/CD credentials, linked to the Mini Shai-Hulud campaign. Socket recommends pinning actions to verified commit SHAs, rotating exposed secrets, and auditing workflow runs since 16 September. (Single source.)
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/compromised-github-actions-came-back.html
