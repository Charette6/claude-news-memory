## Stories

### Contractor malware infection exposes 8.6 million customer records at Japan's largest karaoke operator
Daiichi Kosho disclosed that malware found on an employee computer at its contractor Nippon Columbia Group, which handles its customer data, exposed 8,631,000 customer records and 93,000 employee records (names, genders, birth dates, email addresses, phone numbers). Daiichi says its own systems were not breached and no data theft has been confirmed; no attacker has been named. Single-sourced at time of writing.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/nippon-columbia-malware-incident-exposes-86-million-karaoke-fan-records/

### Booz Allen lab study: autonomous AI agents completed attacks on industrial control systems
Booz Allen Hamilton's OT lab tested two unnamed frontier AI models across eight attack scenarios against a simulated manufacturing environment; the agents achieved the objective in all eight, in one case moving from perimeter compromise to actions inside an industrial control network in just over 16 minutes. Agents received no source code or OT-specific guidance, and required human approval before actions with physical impact.
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/11/ai-systems-are-fully-capable-of-carrying-out-nightmare-attacks-against-infrastructure-and-nobodys-ready/5302450

### Study finds AI coding agents' vulnerability patches often fail project test suites
Northwestern University researchers found that patches produced by AI coding agents frequently stop a crash but fail the maintainers' own tests, with the authors calling such fixes "strictly worse than no fix at all."
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/12/ai-vulnerability-patching-grading-problem/

### CISA adds ProFTPD, ONLYOFFICE Docs, Strapi, Apache Struts and ISC BIND flaws to KEV catalog
CISA added five actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Vulnerability detail is covered in the Daily Vulnerability Brief, not here.
- https://securityaffairs.com/200734/security/u-s-cisa-adds-proftpd-onlyoffice-docs-strapi-apache-struts-and-isc-bind-flaws-to-its-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog.html
