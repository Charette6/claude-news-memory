## Financial sector

### Chainalysis confirms breach by attackers using "offensive agentic techniques"
Blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis confirmed attackers used "offensive agentic techniques" to reach internal systems; exposed data includes customer user IDs, full names, email addresses and organization role labels. The company says it has found no access to investigation content, passwords, MFA secrets or session tokens, and has engaged external investigators.

### Abrigo takes Sageworks lending platform offline after "irregular activity" (single source)
Abrigo, whose Sageworks platform handles loan origination and credit analysis for more than 2,400 US community banks and credit unions, took the service offline on 30 October after detecting irregular activity; borrower-facing features remained down after the weekend. No breach has been confirmed and the company has not said whether data was accessed.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fintech-platform-sageworks-taken-offline-after-suspected-cyberattack/

### FBI attributes $6.3 billion Binance heist to North Korea's TraderTraitor
The FBI attributed the 29 October Binance theft to TraderTraitor (Lazarus-linked) and published wallet addresses for exchanges and financial firms to block. Binance says the intrusion began on a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop; withdrawals have resumed and about 95% of stolen assets have been replenished from reserves.

- https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/binance-hack-test-attack-paths

### Mercado Bitcoin halts withdrawals after cyberattack; $46m reportedly drained (single source)
Brazil's largest crypto exchange (about 4.5 million customers) suspended deposits and withdrawals after confirming a cyberattack; blockchain security firms report more than $46 million left the platform, with roughly $6.5 million since frozen by Tether and other platforms. The company says customer funds were not accessed; researchers citing wallet evidence dispute that.
- https://therecord.media/mercado-bitcoin-brazil-cryptocurrency-heist

## Regulatory & supervisory

### UK NCSC annual review warns of "unprecedented rise" in attacks (single source)
NCSC chief Richard Horne warned of a real prospect of an unprecedented rise in cyber incidents in the coming months, driven by AI industrialising discovery and exploitation of existing vulnerabilities. The NCSC judges it highly likely AI-powered malware will feature in a ransomware attack on UK critical infrastructure by 2028; the UK plans to consult on vendor liability for insecure products.
- https://therecord.media/uk-ncsc-predicts-rise-cyberattacks-coming-months

## Major incidents elsewhere

### Second wave of refrigeration cyberattacks hits European supermarkets
Attackers remotely switched supermarket refrigeration units into defrost mode in Belgium, Luxembourg, Sweden, Estonia and other countries, spoiling stock at chains including Delhaize, Carrefour, Coop and Ica, three weeks after similar attacks in France. Cause: remotely exploitable, internet-exposed management interfaces, many on end-of-life units with no patch. Attribution unknown.
- https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/11/02/frozen-part-ii-hackers-remotely-defrost-produce-in-euro-supermarkets-again/5306924
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/02/european-supermarkets-refrigeration-units-cyberattacks/
- https://securityaffairs.com/202260/security/another-fridge-cyberattack-hits-european-supermarkets-this-time-in-belgium-luxembourg-sweden-and-estonia.html
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-disrupt-refrigeration-systems-at-european-supermarkets/

### Florida student-aid portal breach may affect 4.9 million people (single source)
Florida's Department of Education says hackers breached the state financial-aid website (discovered 19 October, now contained), potentially exposing names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, addresses and contact details of approximately 4.9 million past applicants. No group has claimed the attack.

## Active campaigns

### Vapor Panda uses ARTEX AI framework in automated attacks on 200+ organisations (single source)
CrowdStrike reports the China-nexus actor Vapor Panda used ARTEX AI, a multi-agent penetration-testing framework, against more than 200 organisations in 14 countries since 17 October — sectors include government, telecoms, pharma, semiconductor, defense, nuclear and financial — with at least nine compromised. AI agents performed reconnaissance, exploitation, web-shell deployment and credential theft, with human operators at decision points.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/chinese-hackers-use-artex-ai-in-automated-attacks-against-200-orgs/

### VOLTA infostealer uses commercial AI models to rank victims by financial value
Cisco Talos detailed VOLTA, a Windows infostealer that steals browser passwords, card details, crypto wallet seeds and cloud keys, then sends a victim profile through commercial AI models to grade the victim's worth and security hygiene, abusing stolen Gmail sessions to corroborate home addresses from receipts.
