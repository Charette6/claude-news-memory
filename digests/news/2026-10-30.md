# Security News Digest — 2026-10-30

Public record of security news published in the 24 hours to 2026-10-30 08:03 (UTC+3).

## Financial sector

### Binance investigates suspicious outflows of up to $5.8bn — not yet a confirmed breach
Blockchain analysts flagged large transfers from several Binance hot wallets to new addresses, with Arkham tracking about $5.78bn across 55 addresses tagged to the suspected attacker; Tether and Circle have together frozen more than $2.3bn in linked stablecoins. Binance says it is investigating "reports of a potential security issue" and that user funds are safe — it has not confirmed a hack, and all figures come from third-party chain tracking.

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/binance-probes-potential-58-billion-crypto-theft-hack/
- https://securityaffairs.com/202089/hacking/binance-investigates-suspicious-crypto-transfers-worth-up-to-5-8-billion.html

### Polymarket halted trading after a supplier's web script drained user wallets
Polymarket confirmed that a third-party provider — the performance-monitoring service DebugBear — was compromised to serve wallet-draining JavaScript on its site, hijacking logged-in sessions; researchers traced roughly $2.57m through the attacker's wallet. Trading was halted for about an hour on 29 October and Polymarket says all affected traders will be reimbursed. Single source.

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/polymarket-halts-trading-after-crypto-draining-script-breach-25m-traced/

### Rented Android banking trojan "MR ROBOT" targets bank customers in India
CloudSEK documented a malware-as-a-service platform that rents customised bank-themed Android trojans to affiliates, with about 36 lure apps impersonating banks, telecoms and government services; two tracked affiliate panels showed roughly 300 infected devices and thousands of intercepted OTP messages. The malware steals credentials and card data, intercepts OTPs, and silently enables call forwarding so banks' fraud-check callbacks reach the attacker instead of the customer. Single source.

- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/robot-malware-indian-bank-customers/

## Regulatory & supervisory

### GM pays $220m to 36 US states over selling customer driving data
General Motors settled with a coalition of 36 state attorneys general over enrolling millions of drivers in its Smart Driver programme and selling their location and driving-behaviour data to brokers, who passed it to insurers, between 2016 and 2024. The settlement requires affirmative express consent, data minimisation and an externally assessed privacy programme. Single source.

- https://therecord.media/general-motors-lawsuit-privacy

### NIST publishes migration guidance for post-quantum cryptography deadlines
New NIST guidance helps US federal agencies and their suppliers meet executive-order deadlines of end-2030 for moving high-value systems to post-quantum key establishment and end-2031 for digital signatures. It recommends risk-based prioritisation, migrating long-lived sensitive data first (FIPS 203), considering hybrid encryption, and assessing the full cryptographic supply chain. Single source.

- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/nist-guidance-post-quantum/

## Major incidents elsewhere

### Belgium's tax authority took its filing portal offline after a cyberattack
FPS Finance suspended its main online tax platform MyMinfin on 28 October as a precaution after a cyberattack, days before an end-of-month filing deadline that will now be extended. The agency says there is no indication so far that data was stolen; the attacker and entry route are not yet known.

- https://therecord.media/cyberattack-disrupts-belgian-tax-services-before-deadline
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/29/belgiums-tax-agency-confirms-cyberattack-pulls-services-offline/5306409

### Sony ISP So-net: 2.6 million subscriber records stolen, including some bank details
Sony Network Communications confirmed attackers breached the member database of its Japanese ISPs So-net and Plala on 19 October, detected on 23 October, taking about 2.6 million records: names, dates of birth, contact details, around 54,000 email credentials and 16 bank account records. The incident has been reported to Japan's data protection authority; the attack method has not been disclosed.

- https://www.securityweek.com/hackers-steal-2-6-million-records-from-sonys-japanese-isp-so-net/
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sony-isp-subsidiary-data-breach-exposes-25-million-customer-records/

### Hacktivists publish a searchable database of 41,000 US DHS staff
A hacktivist group released records on 41,191 Department of Homeland Security employees — names, offices, contact details and assignments, including undercover agents — via Distributed Denial of Secrets. The group claims the data came from a DHS Okta single sign-on service; Okta denies its systems were compromised, and only a small sample has been independently verified.

- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/29/icepick-dhs-leak-ice-undercover-agents/
- https://www.securityweek.com/over-41000-dhs-employee-records-leaked-online/

## Active campaigns

### Volt Typhoon's "Silent Train" relay network hid zero-day attacks on network edge gear
Lumen's Black Lotus Labs detailed how the Chinese state group routes its most sensitive intrusions through a constantly rotating relay network of compromised routers and enterprise appliances, exploiting flaws — several as zero-days — in Versa Director, N-able N-central, Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN and VeloCloud Orchestrator against US critical infrastructure, telecoms and service providers, with hundreds of victim notifications over three years. Cisco Talos separately tied exploitation of the Cisco SD-WAN flaw and a key-stealing malware framework called KeyDrift to overlapping infrastructure.

- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/29/volt-typhoon-silent-train-relay-network/
- https://securityaffairs.com/202023/intelligence/volt-typhoon-used-silent-train-to-hide-zero-day-attacks.html
- https://www.securityweek.com/cisco-sd-wan-zero-day-exploitation-tied-to-volt-typhoons-silent-train-network/

### GitHub issues abused to inject commands into CI pipelines of 61 open-source projects
Radware found an attacker who posted commands hidden in invisible HTML comments in GitHub issues and pull requests, which vulnerable GitHub Actions workflows then interpolated into shell steps, exfiltrating secrets and environment variables; 83 payloads hit 61 public repositories including Trivy, MLflow and Brave, most within a 43-minute burst. The researchers could not confirm whether the attacker gained write access or altered any code. Single source.

- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/29/attackers-turn-github-issues-into-supply-chain-campaign-against-61-public-repos-in-less-than-an-hour/5306153
