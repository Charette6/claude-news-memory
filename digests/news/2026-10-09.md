## Financial sector

### South Korean banks lose customer data to AI-automated attacks (`skorea-banks-ai-data-theft`)
CrowdStrike reported that a suspected Chinese-speaking, financially motivated actor used ARTEX, an open-source AI-agent penetration-testing tool driven by commercial LLMs, to breach South Korean financial organisations between late September and early October 2026 and exfiltrate data for sale. Shinhan Bank and Yegaram Savings Bank reported breaches affecting roughly 25,000 and 40,000 people respectively (figures via The Straits Times), and South Korea's Financial Services Commission issued a consumer alert and ordered firms to inspect all externally accessible IT systems.

- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/chinese-hacker-ai-korean-banks/
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/artex-ai-pentesting-tool-used-in-data.html

## Regulatory & supervisory

### PCI SSC publishes guidance on AI systems in payment environments (`pci-ssc-ai-agent-guidance`)
The PCI Security Standards Council published "Security Considerations for AI Systems", recommending explicit human approval for any AI-agent action involving cleartext cardholder data, or tightly bounded "monitored autonomy" with defined permitted actions, shutdown triggers and rollback procedures. The guidance is advisory — existing PCI requirements take precedence — and sets no deadlines. (Single-sourced.)

- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/09/pci-ssc-payment-environments-ai-security-guidance/

### Europol and US GAO press organisations on quantum readiness (`europol-gao-quantum-warnings`)
Europol issued two reports warning that encrypted data stolen today can be decrypted once quantum computers mature ("harvest now, decrypt later"), recommending TLS 1.3, forward secrecy, deletion of unneeded sensitive data and starting post-quantum migration. The US GAO separately reported that none of 24 agencies reviewed had fully addressed its post-quantum preparation recommendations (89 recommendations to 23 agencies). (Single-sourced.)

- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/europol-us-gao-alarm-quantum/

## Major incidents

### Ransomware takes down Japanese cloud provider IDCF Cloud (`idcf-cloud-ransomware`)
A ransomware attack on 7 October shut down the East Japan region of IDCF Cloud, an infrastructure-as-a-service platform run by SoftBank subsidiary IDC Frontier, which confirmed 495 companies and local governments were affected and disabled management consoles across all regions during the investigation. The attacker's claims of 3.6 PB encrypted and 16,000 VM disks sealed are unverified. (Single-sourced.)

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-attack-disrupts-japans-idcf-cloud-used-by-govt-clients/

### Oracle Health breach count climbs toward 20 million (`oracle-health-breach-20m`)
Bloomberg, citing a Texas attorney general report, says close to 20 million people were affected by the early-2025 breach of legacy Cerner systems at Oracle Health, far above earlier filings; Oracle has not confirmed the figure. The original intrusion used stolen customer credentials against a legacy server not yet migrated to Oracle Cloud. (Single-sourced.)

- https://www.securityweek.com/oracle-health-data-breach-tally-climbs-to-nearly-20-million/

## Campaigns

### US seizes Chinese espionage tooling; seven governments issue advisory (`flax-typhoon-tools-seized`)
The US Justice Department seized websites supporting Microscan and FishHub, hacking tools of Beijing-based Integrity Tech, the contractor linked to the Flax Typhoon campaign, while the US, UK, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, Japan and Spain issued a 58-page advisory on six years of Chinese state intrusions. The advisory's central theme is long-term covert access gained through poorly monitored edge devices, with reported victims including utilities and airports.

### Shai-Hulud npm worm spreads via Tensorlake package (`shai-hulud-tensorlake`)
The tensorlake npm package was compromised with the self-propagating Shai-Hulud/ChainDrop credential-stealing worm (malicious version 0.5.144, since removed), which harvests npm, GitHub, AWS and SSH credentials, republishes itself through victims' packages, and persists via planted GitHub Actions workflows and developer-tool hooks. The first rogue commit was pushed on 7 October 2026; no victim count is confirmed.

- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/tensorlake-npm-package-compromised-to.html
- https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/08/shai-hulud-worm-makes-jump-to-ai-infrastructure-with-tensorlake-compromise/5302054

## Worth knowing

### Talos research: AI-analysis evasion in malware (`talos-ai-evasion-research`)
Cisco Talos documented 84 malware samples embedding natural-language instructions aimed at steering AI-based analysis tools; the best techniques shifted verdicts toward benign in about 35% of test runs, though results were inconsistent and conventional detection is unaffected.

- https://blog.talosintelligence.com/ignore-all-instructions-and-read-this-blog-the-state-of-ai-analysis-evasion-in-malware/

### Cisco patches five critical NX-OS flaws (`cisco-nexus-critical-flaws`)
Cisco patched five critical NX-OS vulnerabilities in Nexus 3000/9000 switches allowing root code execution; no exploitation observed. Details covered in the Vulnerability Brief.

- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-warns-of-critical-flaws-allowing-nexus-switch-takeover/
