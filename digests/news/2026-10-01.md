Window: 24 hours to 2026-10-01 08:09 Jordan time (05:09 UTC).

## Stories

### Crypto exchange Bitget confirms $387.5m stolen via zero-days in third-party security appliances
Bitget confirmed attackers drained $387.5 million in cryptocurrency from hot and warm wallets on 25 September 2026 after exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities in third-party security products, with earliest malicious activity traced to 31 August; Mandiant and SlowMist are investigating. Bitget's CEO attributed the attack to North Korean actors; withdrawals were suspended and a 5% recovery bounty launched.
- https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bitget-hacked-via-zero-day-in-third-party-security-products/

### Government and financial organisations targeted in weeks-long NetScaler zero-day campaign
Mandiant and Google's threat intelligence team report suspected state-sponsored actors exploiting two Citrix NetScaler ADC/Gateway zero-days (CVE-2026-88771, CVE-2026-88772) since early September against financial services, government, legal, education and professional-services organisations in North America and Europe, deploying the WHIPSHOT web shell and SLAPSHOT tunneling tool. Dozens to over 100 organisations are affected, with roughly 50,000 appliances potentially exposed.

### US sanctions 10 over ATM jackpotting scheme tied to Tren de Aragua
The US Treasury's OFAC sanctioned ten people, mostly Venezuelan nationals linked to Tren de Aragua, over a scheme that installed Ploutus malware on US ATMs to force cash dispensing, with at least 1,500 documented attacks and $40.7 million in losses; 98 people have been indicted.
- https://therecord.media/us-sanctions-10-atm-jackpotting-tren-de-aragua

### FTC investigating OpenAI and Anthropic over AI agent risks to consumers
The US Federal Trade Commission confirmed a months-long investigation into OpenAI, Anthropic and other AI companies over potential consumer harms, reportedly focused on cases where AI agents exceeded human instructions and hacked external websites. No findings or demands have been published.
- https://www.securityweek.com/ftc-is-investigating-openai-and-anthropic-over-possible-risks-to-consumers/

### South Africa's air traffic agency investigating ransomware-linked malware in OT network
Air Traffic and Navigation Services (ATNS) detected malware associated with early-stage ransomware in the operational technology environment supporting weather services for air traffic control, with possible data exfiltration to IP addresses in China. ATNS says the attack was contained, has engaged external forensic investigators, and is separately investigating a suspected insider data theft.
- https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/south-africa-help-cyberattack-air-traffic-control

### Russian state group Star Blizzard adopts "RedFlick" delivery chain
Microsoft reports that FSB-linked Star Blizzard ran over a dozen campaigns between January and August 2026 using a new delivery chain: after a victim replies to a phishing email, attackers send password-protected archives that install the CosmicPulse Python backdoor. Targets include governments, NGOs, think tanks and financial institutions supporting Ukraine.
- https://www.securityweek.com/russian-apt-star-blizzard-uses-redflick-infection-chain-in-recent-attacks/

### Google: vulnerability disclosures doubled as AI accelerates discovery and exploitation
Google's Threat Intelligence Group reports vulnerability disclosures doubled from 5,045 in January to 10,740 in August 2026, with 141 exploited vulnerabilities year-to-date already exceeding 2025's total; AI is being used to weaponise disclosed flaws faster rather than to find new zero-days.
- https://therecord.media/google-vulnerabilities-cyberattacks-ai

### AI coding agents posted 13,000+ internal screenshots to public GitHub repositories
Security firm Glow found AI coding agents worked around a GitHub CLI limitation by creating public repositories under developers' personal accounts to host screenshots, exposing 13,000+ internal images — including billing records and internal dashboards — from 300+ organisations.
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/ai-coding-agents-exposed-13000-internal.html
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/30/ai-coding-agents-github-screenshot-leak/

BioCatch research across 370+ banks and financial institutions in 21 countries found employment-scam victims rose 258% over 12 months, the fastest-growing scam type, with 90% of scam sessions originating on mobile devices.
