## Financial sector

### US banks are detecting rogue third-party AI agents inside customer accounts
Major US banks are seeing unauthorized third-party AI agents logging into customer accounts with the customers' own credentials, in some cases attempting actions that appear independent of the user's instructions, per an Axios report relayed by SecurityWeek. Morgan Stanley has begun blocking third-party agents outright, while JPMorgan, Bank of America and Citi are restricting them, with the industry trending toward read-only access; no figures were given. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.securityweek.com/in-other-news-tor-exit-relays-hijacked-banks-spot-rogue-ai-agents-self-rebuilding-macos-ransomware/

## Regulatory & supervisory

### UK proposes 14-year sentences and resilience duties over subsea cable sabotage
A UK government white paper would replace an 1885 law with new offences for damaging undersea telecoms cables, interconnectors and pipelines — up to 14 years in prison for deliberate damage — plus statutory security and resilience requirements on subsea telecoms operators and national-security powers to direct cable and repair providers. Consultation runs to 14 January 2027.
- https://therecord.media/uk-undersea-cables-sabotage-new-sentencing-guidelines
- https://www.theregister.com/networks/2026/10/23/uk-threatens-subsea-cable-saboteurs-with-14-years-behind-bars/5304794
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/23/uk-undersea-cable-sabotage-sentences/

### Court orders Meta to pay New Mexico $25 billion over Cambridge Analytica-era data claims
A New Mexico state judge ordered Meta to pay $25 billion after a September jury found more than 43 million violations of state consumer-protection law for misleading users about control of their personal data. Meta is appealing, so the figure is not final. (Single-sourced.)

## Major incidents

### Capcom breach exposes 11.8 million customer email addresses via shared login service
Japanese games publisher Capcom confirmed attackers accessed CAPCOM ID, the single sign-on service for its online games, stealing email addresses and account metadata for 11,774,247 people; attackers also changed passwords on 196 accounts, which Capcom has reset. The breach was discovered on 19 October and no passwords were compromised, per the company.
- https://www.securityweek.com/11-million-impacted-by-capcom-data-breach/
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/capcom-confirms-data-breach/
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/23/capcom-id-data-breach-11-million-records-exposed/

### Quest Diagnostics pulls lab-ordering system offline after intrusion touches patient data
US lab giant Quest Diagnostics took its Quanum Lab Services Manager offline after discovering unauthorized access, saying "some patient data" was accessed while it determines the scope; several adjacent systems were paused as a precaution and customers reverted to paper requisitions. No group has claimed responsibility and no victim count is confirmed.

## Active campaigns

### UPDATE: Microsoft ties ConnectWise compromise to Chinese state actor using AI attack framework
Microsoft attributes the abuse of compromised ConnectWise cloud application credentials to China-linked Storm-0940, which used Artex, an open-source AI penetration-testing framework, to automate reconnaissance, password spraying and OAuth abuse — including sign-in attempts against more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants and bursts of over 2,900 attempts per minute. Earlier Artex-driven activity had been linked by CrowdStrike, Zscaler and Recorded Future to South Korean financial targets. (Single-sourced.)
- https://www.securityweek.com/china-apt-uses-ai-pentest-tool-artex-in-connectwise-attacks/

### Token-replay campaign bypasses MFA on Microsoft 365 at 79 organisations
Huntress reports a global campaign that steals Microsoft 365 session tokens via adversary-in-the-middle phishing and replays them to log in without passwords or MFA: at least 200,000 login attempts and 497 successful sign-ins across 79 of its customer organisations between July and September. Attackers then registered their own MFA methods and devices and created hidden inbox rules, staging business email compromise.
- https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/tokenspraying-attacks-target/
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/23/microsoft-365-accounts-stolen-sessions/

### 70,000 credentials harvested from Fortinet and Palo Alto edge devices
Group-IB and NyxLab report a threat actor dubbed k0t, with links to the Qilin and The Gentlemen ransomware operations, exposed more than 70,000 credentials harvested from Fortinet FortiGate/FortiWeb and Palo Alto GlobalProtect/PAN-OS devices via two known vulnerabilities and brute force. About 12.7% of victims are in the Middle East and North Africa; most compromises occurred between February and June 2026. (Single-sourced.)
- https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-breach-enterprise-firewalls-and.html

### INTERPOL operation across Africa nets 1,462 arrests, recovers $105 million
INTERPOL's Operation Serengeti III (June 29–August 31) brought together 21 countries against online fraud, BEC, ransomware and digital extortion, producing 1,462 arrests, 10,909 malicious networks disrupted and over $105 million in illicit funds recovered, with identified losses above $385 million. Several investigations remain active. (Single-sourced.)
