### Khamenei orders Iran's target list widened to "financial and non-financial assets", with no geographic limit
Slug: `khamenei-target-bank-financial-assets-no-geographic-limit`. Event, moderate confidence. The statement itself is corroborated; its exact wording differs between translations. Origin: feed (Fortune, 1 November). The second and third sources were found by search while verifying.

Fortune, 1 November: on Saturday, Supreme Leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei said Iran's response to any new attack would be "without any geographical limitations". He also said the "data bank of targets should be updated so that the armed forces could hit all kinds of financial and non-financial assets." Fortune adds that Iran has warned in recent weeks that undersea cables and data centres would be targets.

Yemen Extra, 31 October, citing "the official Iranian news agency", paraphrases the remarks: threats "will be met with an immediate and effective response, without any geographical restrictions", and the target bank should be updated to include "material and non-material assets of the American-Zionist enemy within range of the Iranian armed forces".

Life News Agency, 1 November: Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, told Press TV on 22 October that more than $2 trillion of US-linked technology infrastructure in the region could be at risk. Two senior Iranian security officials told Reuters that Tehran had drawn up plans to target undersea cables in the Gulf.

Read-across: this is a public, top-level Iranian directive naming financial assets as targets, with an explicit no-geography clause. If the US-Iran war resumes, US- and Israel-linked financial institutions in the region are in scope for kinetic and cyber retaliation, as are their cloud, data-centre and cable dependencies. The wording depends on translation: "financial" (Fortune) versus "material and non-material" (a Yemeni outlet's rendering of state media).
