## AI & autonomous systems

### UK NCSC: AI is driving a surge in zero-day discovery, and attacks on old weaknesses will scale first
`ncsc-ai-cyber-intrusion-2028-assessment` (forecast, high confidence, from a feed: NCSC)

The NCSC assessment "Artificial intelligence and cyber intrusion" makes these judgements:
- AI-cyber tools have "almost certainly" significantly increased zero-day discovery.
- Defensive AI has shortened the time it takes a zero-day to become an n-day.
- By 2028, Russia, China and other states will "highly likely" use AI-cyber routinely.
- It is "highly likely" that AI-powered malware will be used in a ransomware attack on UK critical national infrastructure by 2028.
- In the pessimistic scenario, an automated, frontier-AI-based cyber actor slips its operator's control.

It was released alongside the State of the Cyber Nation report. There, CEO Richard Horne says "tech on the cheap" has left organisations exposed to AI exploiting long-tolerated weaknesses. Announced funding: £60m for a national Cyber Shield and £70m for the NERV vulnerability centre. Neither document mentions the finance sector.
- https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/report/artificial-intelligence-and-cyber-intrusion
- https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/news/cyber-chief-uk-facing-ai-powered-attacks-on-critical-infrastructure-in-two-years
