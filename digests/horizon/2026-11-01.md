## AI & autonomous systems

### Infostealer sends stolen victim data to commercial AI models to rank who is worth targeting
Slug: `volta-autodoxer-llm-infostealer-triage`. Source: feed (Wired security).

Cisco Talos found an infostealer it calls "Volta-Autodoxer". It collects browser passwords, Microsoft OAuth tokens, cookies, payment cards, crypto seed phrases, and Discord, Telegram and Steam sessions. It then runs a second sweep through the victim's email for receipts and order history. The resulting dossier goes through OpenRouter to an LLM, defaulting to Claude Opus 4.6 with Moonshot Kimi K2 and Gemini 2.5 Flash as fallbacks if the model refuses. A 240-word "forensic intelligence analyst" prompt asks for an assessment of the victim, including what they are "worth". The malware still works without the AI step. Talos flagged it with its new CAIRN framework and did not track whether it has been used in the wild.

Read-across: AI triage of stolen sessions can shorten the time from infection to a targeted account-takeover attempt.

Confidence: moderate (event; single outlet, research shared exclusively with Wired).
