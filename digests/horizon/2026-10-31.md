### A second Chinese state group is now running ARTEX agents, and it breached a Middle East interior ministry
- Slug: `vapor-panda-artex-middle-east-interior-ministry`
- Origin: feed (CyberScoop). The CrowdStrike primary was found through the CrowdStrike blog feed.
- Summary: CrowdStrike reports that VAPOR PANDA, a China-nexus actor, used the ARTEX agentic intrusion framework from 17 October 2026. Its main model was DeepSeek, plus one unidentified commercial LLM. The campaign hit more than 200 organisations in at least 14 countries over an 11-day window. CrowdStrike confirmed post-exploitation at nine organisations; CyberScoop's figure is about 140 compromised. Victims include a Middle East interior ministry (BLOODALCHEMY backdoor), foreign ministries, and 19 US organisations in pharma, semiconductor, defence, nuclear and financial sectors. The operator directed agents to find Windows hosts without an EDR sensor. CyberScoop quotes CrowdStrike's Adam Meyers: hands-on-keyboard activity typically ran up to 40 commands an hour, while ARTEX exceeded 1,500. This campaign is separate from the Storm-0940 ARTEX activity Microsoft reported earlier.
- Read-across: full EDR coverage including internal servers, SAML signature validation, IMDSv2, rotating signing keys, and hunting for "artx"/"artex" markers.
- Type and confidence: event, high.
- Sources: https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/vapor-panda-uses-artex-to-scale-global-campaign/ ; https://cyberscoop.com/china-vapor-panda-agentic-ai-artex-campaign-crowdstrike/

### An extortion crew with no exploits used a 154-model AI coding setup to build its tooling
- Slug: `socradar-ai-assisted-extortion-crew-154-models`
- Origin: feed (The Register).
- Summary: SOCRadar found an operator's exposed file server in Sweden holding 1,719 files. The operator used the OpenCode agent with 154 model entries across seven providers, and AI spend passed $7,216 by 22 October. The models wrote and debugged attack scripts; SOCRadar found no evidence of a fully autonomous system. Initial access came from RDP scanning, FortiGate SSL-VPN credential stuffing and exposed cloud credentials. Of 23 targeted organisations, named victims include a Netherlands-based financial services provider and an e-commerce firm whose bank-transaction archives were deleted.
- Type and confidence: event, moderate.
- Source: https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/30/crim-spent-7k-on-ai-services-in-extortion-attacks-then-left-evidence-for-all-to-see/5306754
