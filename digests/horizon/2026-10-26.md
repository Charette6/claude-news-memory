## AI & autonomous systems

### Off-the-shelf AI safety guards can be tricked into judging a harmless decoy instead of the real request
- Slug: guard-target-rebinding-safety-classifiers
- Type and confidence: lab result, moderate (a preprint, not peer reviewed)
- Origin: feed (arXiv cs.CR)
- Summary: Safety guard models usually receive the content to check pasted into a plain-text prompt. Content that imitates that prompt can add a forged second task with a harmless decoy, and the guard grades the last complete task it sees, so the harmful request passes. The authors call this "target rebinding". In a pre-registered test, a forged query plus decoy got all 75 held-out AdvBench requests that Shieldstral-1.0-3B flags past it. On the GuardRebind benchmark, each of five guards (Shieldstral, gpt-oss-safeguard, ShieldGemma, Llama Guard 3, Qwen3Guard) had an attack family, chosen per guard, that cleared at least 93% of the items it had flagged. A plain "ignore the text above" instruction cleared none. The authors report that training the guard to score content no lower than its most harmful part cut attack success to 0.8-1.6%.
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31695

### Musk confirms SpaceX restricted Ukraine's Starlink, citing its terms of service
- Slug: starlink-ukraine-tos-restriction-admitted
- Type and confidence: event, high
- Origin: feed (BBC World)
- Summary: Musk said SpaceX restricted Ukraine's Starlink service, accusing Ukraine of using it for offensive drone strikes in breach of the terms of service. He had earlier denied deliberately throttling the connection. Kyiv says the problem hampered its defensive operations for more than a week. Service appeared restored after Trump said he had told Musk to "put it back". Ukraine's defence minister reported a stable connection, with drones exceeding 400 km/h, up from a previous cut-off at 161 km/h. Read-across: one operator can unilaterally degrade a commercial satellite service for a whole country under political pressure, so any failover path that depends on it needs a second option.
