## Geopolitical & regional

### UPDATE: Iran tables a seven-day roadmap to reopen Hormuz and open talks with the US

Slug: `us-iran-unga-talks` (update to an entry first seen 2026-09-24)

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi presented a seven-day plan outside the UN Security Council on 25 September. Initial steps would take four to five days, the Strait of Hormuz would reopen on day six, and talks with the US "for a final deal on mutually agreed subjects" would begin on day seven. Iran's asks: the US lifts its naval blockade of Iranian ports, waives sanctions on Iranian oil sales, releases roughly $12 billion in frozen assets, and a ceasefire takes hold across the region including Lebanon and Yemen. A White House official told CNN the administration was having "positive and constructive discussions through the mediators"; no acceptance has been announced. Oman and Iran discussed Hormuz navigation on the UNGA sidelines. In a CBS interview President Pezeshkian said Iran would allow UN nuclear inspectors to return under accords reached through negotiation.

Why it matters: a deal would move Iran sanctions screening and correspondent-banking exposure within days. The comparable 17 June agreement collapsed within weeks over control of shipping through Hormuz, so nothing is settled until the US answers.

Confidence: event, moderate.

### Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia continue

Slug: `houthi-strikes-riyadh-yanbu` (continuation of an entry first seen 2026-09-25)

Coalition spokesman Col. Turki Al-Maliki said on 26 September that air defences intercepted a ballistic missile launched toward Khamis Mushait, and earlier the same day two drones heading for Riyadh. Saudi Arabia said the "danger has passed" in Jazan province. Separately, Middle East Eye reports the Houthis wrote to the EU saying they do not intend to target European vessels and that their Red Sea blockade applies only to Saudi Arabia and Yemen's internationally recognised government.

What would make it matter: the Yemen ceasefire clause in Iran's roadmap failing and strikes widening beyond Saudi territory.
