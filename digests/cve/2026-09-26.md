### CVE-2026-87902 - WordPress Core

- CVSS: 8.1 (v3.1) / 9.2 (v4 per advisory) | EPSS: 0.029 | KEV: yes

Affects 4.7.0-7.1.1; fixed in 7.1.2 with backports 4.7.37+. KEV added 2026-09-25, due 2026-09-28, ransomware use: Unknown. Patchstack observed probing within hours of the patch.
